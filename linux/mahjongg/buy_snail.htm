By snail mail

You may purchase the game via snail mail, although this is by far the slowest way. We accept either cheque, credit card or cash.

If you have an email address, please include it on the form as doing so will enable us to email you a license code as soon as payment has been processed.

When paying by cheque make all monies payable to "Small Rockets".

Paying by cash

We do not recommend payment in cash. We do not take any responsibility for any cash sent to us that is not sent by recorded delivery and recorded as received by us. Thus it is entirely at your own risk if you wish to send us payment via mail without recorded delivery.

Please note:

  • We only accept payment in American Dollars or UK Pounds Sterling.
  • You should place any money in an opaque envelope.
  • You must enclose a completed order form (see below) with your money

What to do with the license code

Once you receive your code, follow these steps:

Go to /usr/local/games/smallrockets/<gamename>/
type:

./setlicense

This will launch the key program where you can enter your name and the license key to unlock the full version.

Enter this information and your demo version of 'Small Rockets Mah Jongg' will automatically become the full version of the game.
