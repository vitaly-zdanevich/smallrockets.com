A game of skill and luck...

The solitaire version of Mah Jongg is a game of matching pairs of tiles. A stack of tiles lie on the table and it is necessary to match two of one type in order to remove them from the stack.

The game is complete when all the tiles have been removed, clearing the table. However it is only possible to remove two matching tiles if they are "free". A free tile is one which has nothing touching its left or right side.
