Friday, February 19, 2010
 
I call on the Government to produce a National Strategy for Motor Neurone Disease (MND). MND is a devastating condition which wrecks lives and kills five people every day in the UK. But no national guidance exists to set standards of care for people with MND and without this, care can be fragmented, inaccessible, untimely and of poor quality. A National Strategy is urgently needed to ensure equity of access to services and to enable people with MND to achieve the highest quality of life possible and to die with dignity.
