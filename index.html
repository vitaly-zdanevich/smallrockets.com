Small Rockets Is Shutting Down

From: Jonathan Small

Unfortunately all good things must eventually come to an end.

It's a gut wrenchingly sad thing for me to have to write this and say that it's all over. I've put a great deal into the 'Small Rockets' project over the past 12 years. We have had some amazingly talented people pass through our doors. It's been many years now since we've had an office in Guildford, with the teams and people that made the games and the culture that defined the company. I can only smile as I think back to those early days.

Why now? If you've been following the forums, you will have seen the ongoing work on Red Ace Squadron 2, and the engine. An impressive amount has been achieved, but it has become clear that the target is like a mirage - always just over the horizon. Finally despite the progress I have to conceed that realisically it's not going to happen - theres just way to much to do, and I just can't make it all happen.

I'd like to thank all the Small Rocketers for all their hard work. It was great knowing you and working with you. I'd also like to thank the community around Small Rocket games, and in particular Red Ace Squadron. Their passion and effort was a key driver behind trying to keep the dream alive. In no particular order I'd live to thank xray and fzredbaron, geometrian, moose, RAFcaptain, KrimsonAce, Falling Guy as well as all the people who contributed to the community and to the games.

Finally I'd like to thank Mr Sean Baggaley who has provided support on the website and the forums for years. He's an old school friend of mine and I really appreciate all he's done for Small Rockets.

Jonathan Small,
Managing Director,
Small Rockets/Small Software Ltd
