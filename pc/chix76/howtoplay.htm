How to play

Superchix 76 has 2 game modes - Rally Race and Cop Chase.

In Rally Race mode, it's you against the other Chix - you'll need to score the most points in the championship to proceed to the next. New tracks will become available as you win championships.

In Cop Chase mode, you're one on one against one of the other Chix. Easy, huh? There's a catch - the boys in blue haven't taken too kindly to your speeding antics, and will do everything they can to slow you down. Winning championships in Rally Race mode will open up new tracks in Cop Chase mode.

Controls

Chix can be played with the keyboard, a steering wheel or a joystick:

Up,AAccelerate/Forward
Down,ZBreak/Reverse
LeftLeft
RightRight
SpaceHand brake
F2Switch camera view
F11Save screen shot to disk
EscIn game menu

To fine tune your steering, try tapping the left and right keys.
Holding the left or right keys down when travelling at high speed will automatically send the car into a power slide, an essential skill if you're going to beat the other Chix.
You can pull off even more severe power slides by using the handbrake. Although this will slow you down a bit, your car will swing round corners far more than just steering ever would!

Steering Controls

Superchix 76 is compatible with steering wheel controllers. Simply enter the 'Options' menu and set the 'Control' option to Wheel. Once this is done the game can be controlled using the steering wheel and pedals, with the handbrake assigned to one of the buttons on the wheel.

Joystick/Gamepad Controls

Superchix 76 can be controlled using a joystick/gamepad with at least 4 buttons. The buttons used by the game are

Button 2 - Brake

Button 3 - Accelerate

Button 4 - Handbrake

The control buttons cannot be altered within the game. If you wish to alter the functions of the buttons you will need to alter their settings within Windows (please consult your controllers documentation for details).
