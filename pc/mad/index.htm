MAD: Global Thermonuclear Warfare

Price:$14.99 
Format:ESD only (more)
Genre:Strategy
Multiplayer: Yes
Windows  

Description

Space stations ring the earth. Nuclear arsenals are being amassed. Diplomacy has failed.

Only one superpower can be victorious....

MAD is an arcade strategy game of apocalyptic proportions. Build up your bases, arm your satellites and research more and more powerful nukes as you attempt to outmaneuver a wily opponent. There are no prizes for second place - only total destruction of your enemy will guarantee success.

Full version features

  • No limit on destroyed territories - play through to the bitter end!
  • Five grades of nuke, ranging up to the awesome destructive capability of the Minuteman X
  • Multiplayer mode - challenge another power-mad dictator via LAN or the Internet
  • 3 levels of difficulty, with devilish computer AI
  • Ability to research and deploy the full range of weaponry, including Disruptors, Tesla Pulses and the Doomsday Device
