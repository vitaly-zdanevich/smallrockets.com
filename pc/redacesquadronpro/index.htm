Description

Red Ace Squadron Pro (RAS Pro) has been created for, and with the help of, the RAS community. The focus of the update has been on the multiplayer game - with many new multiplayer modes and playing styles now available. Additionally there are 3 new difficulty modes in the single player game.

Best news of all, RAS Pro is being made available for FREE to all customers who have purchased RAS from SmallRockets website (www.smallrockets.com) - just use your licence key from RAS and it will work automatically with Pro. If you purchased RAS from elsewhere you will need to contact your vendor for their policy on RAS Pro.

Overview

Fly a World War I bi-plane, and experience aerial combat in its purest form. No jets, no computers, no heat-seeking missiles - just the wind in your struts and blazing machine-guns!

Red Ace Squadron is the follow-up to the best-selling Master Of The Skies: The Red Ace. You won't need to learn complex key commands or read a huge manual to play - your plane is controlled solely by the mouse and a few other keys.

Play for either the Allied Forces or the Central Powers across 14 new single-player missions, or take on human opponents in the all-new Multiplayer mode. Whether you choose to play online or alone, Red Ace Squadron is the ultimate propellor-driven dogfighting experience.

Full version features

  • Multiplayer update on the classic Red Ace Squadron
  • Cheat protected, configurable server, more levels of difficulty and much more!
  • Follow Allied or Central Powers campaigns in single player, with 14 new missions
  • Power-up stations allow players to re-arm and repair
  • Pilot two new twin-engined bombers - the Handley Page and the Gotha G IV
  • Complete campaigns to access special super-hard Master Mode, and a secret mission!
