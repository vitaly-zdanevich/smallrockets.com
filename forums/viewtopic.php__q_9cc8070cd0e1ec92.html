1. Different game modes for more or less realism.
a. Team Mode for MP gamers (Allied vs Central Powers)
b. Choice of no rockets &/or Boost modes.
c. Limited ammunition & fuel (landing required not just fly-bys).
d. Choice of more SP "timed" sorties.

2. Additional airplane choices:
a. 2 seater planes (RAF RE8, Sopwith 1 1/2 Strutter, etc.) w/ computer controlled gunner for SP mode & 2 gamers for MP mode.
b. Plane artwork editor - making planes more recognizable in MP mode.

3. Improved map identifiers.
a. Better individual plane altitude indicators. (not just grays)

4. Improved graphics (better details)
5. Improved game controller interfacing for gamepads & joysticks w/ "Force Feedback".

My 2 pennies worth.
Criticisms & further suggestions?

Yes, it is possible to land a plane on an airstrip or surrounding ground. It can also be hard to come to a complete stop at times but, these are some of the challenges that makes a game fun. It is necessary to develop flying skills to become a proficient aviator.

On the subject of armaments -- Limiting amounts of armaments puts thinking back into a game. If machine-gun rounds are unlimited it creates an atomosphere where everyone flying sprays the skies with bullets hoping to hit another plane. By limiting the number of bullets that can be carried at one time a flyer is suddenly faced with the possibility of using up his/her ammunitions and being shot down. Using ammunition wisely is introduced into the game. No longer will aviators become Aces by simply taking aim on the whole sky.

My 2 more pennies.

Yes, I'm not interested in RAS2 becoming a complicated flight simulator such as "Combat Flight Simulator" (tm. Microsoft) or Sturmovik IL-2 (tm. UbiSoft), either. I have enjoyed RAS, too, because it represents the era when flying by the seat of one's pants and barnstorming was high technology. -- And, I think RAS2 should retain those qualities from RAS(1).

I don't want to have to "balance out" my munitions load, worry with controlling independent wing surfaces (ailerons, elevator, or rudder), or figure out what air-to-fuel mixture is best. Rather, I would enjoy RAS2 if I was able to grab my joystick, use its throttle lever effectively to control engine speed and be able to have enough control over my Sopwith Camel to successfully fly a "Split-S" or "Immelmann" attack so, that I might compete with the Fokker "Triplane" or Junkers "Scout" mp gamers without having to worry that another Allied plane is trying to shoot my tail off. Or, that an enemy plane in my sights is going to zoom away from eminent destruction because he/she is in reality a disguised rocketeer rather than an aviator. and, finally, that a Maverick missile isn't going to take me down 100 yards beyond the flight-line.

You bring up a good point Bospqr about radar in a Sopwith Camel. I hadn't thought about it but, by blanking out the radar and making it a compass/map only in mp mode it would require a pilot to visually recognize a plane type before engaging the machine-guns. This might cause fewer planes being in a particular dogfight. It would also be necessary to visually find the battle rather than checking the radar first. Rogue pilots like game cheaters would have a short life, too.

Just my 6 cents (pence) worth.
