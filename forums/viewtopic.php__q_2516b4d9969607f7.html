Allied Mission NO.3 Master Mode

by Frank Parkinson on Wed Apr 30, 2003 7:48 am

Hi,

We currently have two of the best RAS players in the world working up a script for the completion of the game, plus some instructions for becoming an online master of the skies. To be honest I missed out completely working on RAS. I've been away doing other (less fulfilling) things for two years and I've only recently rejoined the company.
So I'm only able to supply the experience of others from writings I've found on the forums, or in the technical support e-mails.
To that end, here's one example of writing on this particular subject:

"But.... I don't really know how I did it. The only thing I know is that I didn't end up in a confrontation with the deadly flame plane.

I don't know if the other fighters took him out or what. I guess that must be the case.

I start out and take out the closest gun on top of the plateau with my guns, then circle and take out the two planes, then by diving and going at the other two guns and releasing a bomb just as I crest the top, I can take out the other two. (this takes practice)

Then I take out the three guns that are closest to the plateau and take on any planes that come at me. I try to high-tail it back to the circle area after taking out guns as then you have the cover of the mountains and by swooping and circling, you can usually take them out without much damage.

Then I go back near the beginning and fly at the fork in the road directly at the right height that the two planes appear. If you get it right, you can take out the first plane right away and then get to the second before he wheels around on you.

Then I take out a gun on the inside of the first fort, then fight it out with the planes that appear.
After that, I take out the other two guns and destroy the tanks. The german bombers usually have appeared by now.

Then it's tricky. I try to help out by taking out some more guns and try to avoid getting swarmed on.

If you hang back near the center, the flame guy might get downed, but even if not, he'll have to come at you and you stand a better chance in the mountains and valleys rather than on the flats."

We hope to have the solution on the website before the end of May.

Thanks,
Frank Parkinson
Small Rockets

Hi,

Whoops, sorry - long hours and lack of attention to detail (no excuse, really) - I'm assured by everyone here that the key to this mission in Master Mode, is getting to the Gotha before the first plane attacks. If unsuccesful, this attacker will take the professors plane down.
If you get the first attacker you need only repeat your actions from easy mode thereafter.
