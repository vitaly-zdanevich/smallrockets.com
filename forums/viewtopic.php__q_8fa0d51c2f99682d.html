RadarSync

by BeachBum on Sun Jan 29, 2006 10:18 pm

Cool... Hope it works out as advertised. At first blush, it seems to be at least on par with others I know of, like PC Pitstop, VersionTracker Pro, and Drivers Headquarters.

Of course, for me, what I like about RadarSync is the FREE part. What it also does for me is give me a quick and dirty free check of my friend's PCs when called on to get them out of a jam. My experience has been the more adamant someone is about all their drivers and software being up to date, the worse I find things to actually be... go figure.

BTW, if you use Firefox, you will need to switch the browsing engine to IE, for FireFox to allow ActiveX to run, or you could just use IE, but I would never recommend opening that gaping security hole to anyone.

You also may need to remember any special things you may have done with your PC. For instance, I always use Omega drivers for my video cards, instead of the original manufacturer's drivers. These driver checkers always report that I need the latest update of the original manufacturer's drivers. Ya' gotta remember not to do that.

And this puppy even checks for BIOS and firmware updates.

Clear left,
