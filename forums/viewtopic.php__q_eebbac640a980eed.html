New Forum Software

Posted: Mon Jan 28, 2008 10:35 am

As you can probably tell, the forum software has been (massively) updated. With luck, this should finally spell the end of spamming on the forums.

Jon still has some cosmetic touches to complete, (such as restoring the Small Rockets template), but the forums themselves are now up and running again.

NEW FEATURES:

* Proper moderation system. This enforces a "moderator queue", to which all new posts will be sent.
- This lets me screen all posts from new users, thus eliminating spam.

* Initially, _all_ posts are being sent to the moderation queue, so my apologies to any regulars wondering why their posts aren't appearing right away! I'll pop you into the unmoderated users group as soon as I can. (Jon and I are a bit snowed under with work, so I admit this may take a day or so.)

* Vastly improved user control panels, templates, back-end features and speed improvements.

* Basically, it's better, faster and much easier to administrate and post on. This should make for a much friendlier environment.
