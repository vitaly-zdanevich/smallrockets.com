Lalla...

Assuming you have WINDOWS XP but nothing less than WINDOWS 2000, ME, etc.....

CLICK >START>SETTINGS>CONTROL PANEL>ADD OR REMOVE PROGRAM..... then find Red Ace Squadron or Red Ace Squadron Pro and CLICK "REMOVE". That is all there is to it.

IF, however, this fails........

CLICK >START>PROGRAMS>ACCESSORIES>WINDOWS EXPLORER. Now, CLICK on WINDOWS EXPLORER, go to MY COMPUTER and EXPAND the FOLDER. CLICK on LOCAL DISK(C) and EXPAND THE FOLDER. Go to PROGRAM FILES and EXPAND THE FOLDER. Go down to SMALL ROCKETS FOLDER. Now... RIGHT CLICK with your MOUSE and SELECT "DELETE".

This is a long way of telling you how to do it.... but given that your English doesn't appear to be great.... should make it easier for you to understand.

Good Luck!
