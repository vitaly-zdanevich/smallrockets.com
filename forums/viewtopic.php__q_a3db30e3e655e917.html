Hehe, those are great games, but... not all are vertical scrolling (don't really care, I actually prefer horizontal scrolling games like R-Type and Pulstar), .... and .... technically playing those games on mame is illegal unless you actually own the roms yourself.

I think the previous poster asked more about shooters specifically for the PC. The selection gets a little smaller there, but here are a few (not necessarily scrolling) shmups to try out:

1. Raptor (now for Windows, the grand-daddy of PC shmups. I find it kinda slow, but others quite like it)
2. Intensity XS (relatively new, very simple gameplay, but highly addictive)
3. Bugatron (kinda like Galaga with 3d graphics)
4. Space Tripper (a bit defenderish, quite fun)

Hope this helps.
