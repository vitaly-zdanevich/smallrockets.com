by Geometrian on Wed May 30, 2007 6:54 am

Hey, you two, do you ever get a strange error on the secret mission (sometimes on other missions too) where if you are in the middle of a dogfight and you restart, then fly to where the dogfight happened, you see yourself completely motionless? I get this error on the secret mission like this:

1. Take off and fly to Hermione.
2. Get immersed in a huge dogfight; evade the enemies' fire but stay with them.
3. Veer off and fly away from the battle; the enemies will follow you.
4. Slow down until you are being constantly shot by the enemies.
5. Remember your position.
6. Restart.
7. Fly to your position and if you did it right you should see yourself comletely static.

Sometimes, though, this doesn't work at all and you should start over at step 1.

These "stillplanes" have interesting properties. They don't move, can't be hit by the enemy, can't be hit by your bullets, can be hit by your secondary ordinance (such as rockets or bombs), can change colour to red (like you do when you're hit); but not back to normal, flying through them makes you hurt, etc.

I say this because using this technique over several iterations, one can make as many nieuports as you want. (I got 15).

Does any of that sound familiar?
