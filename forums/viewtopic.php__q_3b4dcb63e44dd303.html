I have found the easiest way to connect is through GameSpy which is a free download. You can go there and join one of their games then disconnect then click "change server" then write that number down. You then can quit GameSpy then click on your RAS icon, click multiplayer, click change IP server, type in the number you have from GameSpy. This IP seems to be in use most of the time
67.112.200.123, another IP is 65.78.112.54. If you connect through RAS multiply your ID is not exposed like it is on GameSpy.

Hope this helps, by the way, you usually have to click internet connect at least 3 times for the same IP.
