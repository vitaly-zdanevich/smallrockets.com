RAS 2 development

by jonathan on Wed Aug 15, 2007 3:02 am

Hi,

Sean has been telling me I've been rather lax at getting to the forums, and unfortunately he is right. So I'm here to say hi, and give a quick update.

In terms of development things are moving along. Unfortunately RAS2 isn't the only thing I have to work on so, its only part of my time I can devote to its development and thats one of the reasons its taking longer than expected.

The other side of the story is that there really have been two developments of RAS2. The screen shots you can see of RAS2 and the editor were with the new engine and editor developed post Ultra Assault. Now RAS2 is being developed on new technology. The old technology wasn't bad per se, but the idea is to develop a technology base that could be used for all new SR developments, and its wasn't really up to scratch..

So the development continues, in fits and starts. Much of the tech is there. The communities suggestions have certainly set the bar high, and its my every intention to try and make them a reality in the new game.

As Sean says I'm loathed to throw out dates, as I've been so wrong before. I know thats a bit weak - but let it be known, you'll know here first. Beta versions will be here first, and when I have a solid date - when I can give that - it will be here.

Anyways I wanted the community to know that development is continuing and to that you all for your support and interest.

There is another team developing a new SR game also. More on that at a later date

Thanks again all, especially for your understading and patience,

Jonathan
