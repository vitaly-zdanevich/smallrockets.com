by fzredbaron on Tue May 24, 2005 12:37 pm

Although it has taken a great deal of effort on Jonathan's part (because the original RAS program is somewhat outdated computer code wise), I am proud to announce that through about a dozen (12) hidden different sub-routine programs, RAS1 PRO will be CHEAT FREE! The program KNOWS what the FACTORY SETTINGS ARE and if anyone tries to LOG ON to a RAS1 PRO SERVER and has ALTERED ANYTHING in their program that has to do with the OBJECTS... Ordinance Settings, Plane Dynamics, etc., the RAS1 PRO SERVER will BLOCK THEIR ACCESS.... PERIOD! And being one who HATES the "Trainer" more than anyone... that is in the TOILET ALSO! Of course, if someone does not HAVE the RAS1 PRO UPGRADE, they won't be able to get in either!

And just in case you wondered, YES.... you can still make changes to the JOYSTICK CONFIG FILES and YES..... you can still PAINT your PLANES. The Anti-Cheat Security System only applies to GAME SETTINGS.

And of course, this will NOT affect a HOST being able to set up their own SERVER OPTIONS the way that they want. But regardless of HOW someone sets up their OWN HOST SERVER SETTINGS.... when they go into someone else's HOST SERVER, the HOST SERVER SETTINGS that they are playing in RULE..... regardless of what the PLAYER may have set on theirs. Got It.... hehe!

The RAS1 PRO launch is forecasted for the first week of June.

RAS 1 Pro = Awesome game

by Tater on Sat May 28, 2005 7:37 pm

As a tester for the new version, RAS1 Pro, only one word comes to mind, AWESOME. One of the hardest habits to break after over 4 years of playing this fantastic game, was not running to the power ups upon spawning. When you find yourself in the seat of the cockpit, you are ready to fly. Rockets and boost on game default are already loaded and at your disposal. When your wheels are off the ground you need only to seek your target and engage warfare. There will be no more circling the power ups waiting for them to respawn so you can load up. No more hard feelings that you were shot down and didn't even get to load for a fair dogfight. A person serving the game will be able to set parameters on power ups and their respawn time. This new version will be password protected, if one wants it that way. The 8 player windows in the GSA room window will accurately show the amount of players that are truely playing, no more trying to find which room has players in it. The RAS1 Pro community owes a big thank you to Jonathan, for his time and efforts. They owe a big (salute) to FZ Red Baron for his time and hard work also, and last, but not least a big thank you to XRAY who has spent his time with others to numerous to mention. The best thing to mention is that this is all being given away FREE,to those who have the full game of Red Ace Squadron. So again I say thank you Jonathan and FZ for your time, I know the whole RAS1 Pro community feels just like I do.
