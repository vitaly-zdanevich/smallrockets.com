by stimarco on Sun Oct 21, 2007 11:04 am

tim the ace wrote:What's the difference between the four difficulty levels in Red Ace Squadron Pro?


The enemies are more perfect, the harder the level.

Making enemies in games completely perfect is technically very easy, but it makes for a dull game. The trick is to modify the enemies' behaviour so that they make realistic mistakes without looking like complete idiots. This is a tricky balancing process: one gamer's "easy" is another gamer's "hard". (The "Red Ace" games are very hard for me to play, regardless of the difficulty setting.)

In a dogfighting game, the trick is to stop the planes acting as if they know exactly what you're doing even when, in theory, they shouldn't be able to see you. They should act surprised when you dive on them from out of the sun; they ought to scatter and regroup in a tactically realistic fashion, and so on. Unfortunately, the original "Red Ace" engine was built by programmers who weren't all that familiar with dogfighting tactics, (none of them had ever flown a real plane), so the programming will probably seem quite basic to any real-world dogfighting or aerobatics experts. If this is the case for you, I can only apologise!
