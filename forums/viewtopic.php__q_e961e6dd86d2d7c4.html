Games they want

Posted: Mon Feb 17, 2003 5:55 pm
by Shah Amin
We at Cyber6 club have done a poll with our members,about what kind of games they want to see as a simulation game:


1.Tank sim.- Modern day tank game where you can set the drive as automatic and control the turret or control both, tank could ranged between T-55 to T-90 for the Warsaw pack and M1A1 to Leopard A4 for the NATO side, with urban tank battles!.

2.Submarine sim.- A more improved 'small rocket' version of the Famous Silent hunter series, with less file size for wide range of gamer from modem to ADSL.

3.Tank sim.-WWII Tank simulation, need I say more?!.

4.Sailing ship sim.- Age of pirates and cut throats ,like a mixtures of the 'Corsair', 'Age of sail' and 'cutthroats' game, a FREE to choose side game with handful of ships to choose and customize it's canons crew etc,.

5.WWI Tank sim.-A game about WWI, that's more horrific and troublesome war, with gas and chemical agents and flame throwers are widely used for the 1st time!.

From feedbacks that we get from our members, Small Rocket team is one of the top 5 shareware game developer ,in terms of quality(graphics) and practicality(file size), Most big companies does not put enough effort in shareware games, we hope the Small Rockets team would consider our suggestions.
