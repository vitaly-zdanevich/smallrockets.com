Sometimes, the bomber will just fly away for some reason after you get it to the base. If that happens, I guess you just have to restart the mission. I'll post step-by-step instructions as well as I remember on how to get to that point. Take off and fly northeast as shown on the compass. You should encounter an AA gun by itself. Destroy it and fly on. Next, you will find a plane sitting on a runway on a tall island. Fly to the left of this plane and try to stay as far away from it as possible. You cannot disturb a single plane on the ground. If you do, it will come after you, and you'll need to kill it and get over your kill limit. Destroy the two AA guns to the left of the plane and then keep flying past the AA gun and destroy two more AA guns on the same island. Then, turn to the right and destroy a single AA gun but be careful not to fly onto the radar of a plane on the ground. Don't fly close enough to a plane to let it get off the edge of your radar unless it is in the air already. AA guns don't count as enemies. You can destroy as many of them as you like. After you destroy that AA gun, turn north, and you should come across another one pretty quickly. Destroy that one and turn east. Soon, you'll come to the professor's plane and an enemy plane. Destroy the enemy plane but make sure the professor doesn't get too far ahead of you. Next, the professor will fly right next to the enemy plane on the hill. Don't fly as close as the professor is flying. Keep that plane on the edge of your radar! Right past this plane, another will fly over and attack the professor. Destroy this plane as quickly as possible! It has rockets and will quickly down the professor. After this, there are no more enemy planes until you reach your base. When you and the professor reach the base, two planes will appear immediately. One of these will attack you, while the other attacks the professor. Take down them both. After this, the professor will start circling, and another plane will appear and attack him while he's wasting time. Just destroy that plane, and the professor should fly over your base and end the mission soon. Also, another plane will come after you from back where the professor came from sometime while you're escorting the professor. Just destroy that one as quicky as possible while staying with the professor. This is six planes already flying that you can destroy. If you destroy any more, you'll fail the mission.
