by stimarco on Fri Sep 14, 2007 8:09 pm

Dash234 wrote:hello? anybody there? its been quiet for a long tome now HELLO?


We're a bit tied up at the moment. The forum's giving us spam headaches again: I'm killing spams every day now, even though I only get paid to do support three days a week. So it looks like the forum software will need work yet again, which is a royal pain.

This means imaging the server, shutting it down, installing the new version, upgrading the old database to match, hacking the new forum code to work well with the rest of the site, then monitoring it all to make sure no unexpected glitches show up. This process can take a couple of days, effectively wiping out a whole week's work given that SR is only a part-time project for Jon at present. (He has a day-job to make ends meet, which is why he's so rarely seen on these forums.)

I can't think of any alternative for this; we can't allow pornographic images to stay on the forums, but there's not much we can do with the existing software, which doesn't support full post moderation. If the upgrade doesn't work as hoped, other measures will be needed, which will only waste more time.

There is also an increasing demand for Vista compatibility for all our titles. This will also take valuable time away from RAS 2 work, but Jon cannot leave this much longer as the increased sales could also contribute to the RAS 2 development budget.

At any rate, it is looking more likely that RAS 2 will not be out before 2008 now. This forum spam problem has already wasted over two months this year. Its resurgence isn't something either of us welcome. If anyone ever needed evidence that spam isn't really free, this is it.

Sorry folks. I wish I could give you more positive news.

by stimarco on Sun Sep 16, 2007 5:49 pm

ton1 wrote:[color=red]BULLS**T[/color]
...

It looks to me like it will never be released, if that is the case you may as well take down the site and stop building peoples hopes up


Firstly, RAS 2 has only been in development since 2004. I don't know why people think it's been in the works for longer, because it hasn't. Three years isn't _that_ long for a one-man development process. Especially as the original "Red Ace" games took whole teams to create.

At no point has Jon EVER claimed that RAS 2 would DEFINITELY be completed this year. He is no happier about this dragging on for so long than you are: he doesn't get paid for RAS 2 until it's released and SR certainly isn't pulling in much money at the moment. (Hence the day-job.) "When it's ready" is the only specific 'deadline' Jon has made any commitment to. It ain't ready.

It is much closer to release than it was last year, but it's not there yet and spam, webserver problems and the need to make all the old SR games Vista-compatible are not helping matters.

Finally, if you'd just paid for RAS Pro, or any other SR title, and suddenly the website disappeared forever, how would you feel? Shutting down SR overnight will mean no "Red Ace" games *at all*, because modifying the old game engine to work without the licensing system isn't a trivial process.

Jon isn't *just* working on RAS 2. He's building a completely new set of tools to build *all* SR's future games with, but which can also be used to rebuild SR's older titles -- including RAS Pro -- to make them much easier to port to other platforms and maintain.
