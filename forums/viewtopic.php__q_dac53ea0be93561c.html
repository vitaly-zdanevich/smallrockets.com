by stimarco on Thu May 03, 2007 1:33 pm

Zaniumz wrote:yes I have and I also disabled the built in firewall but no luck I seem to be able to host games as well but nobody can join


Odd. There has to be something blocking the ports somewhere between you and the internet, but I can't think of what it might be other than a firewall or your router.

Have you followed Xtra's advice on opening ports ("pinholes" as they call it) on your router? Their page can be found here. (At least, I'm assuming this is your provider.)

The other issue may be that you don't have a Static IP Address. It doesn't look like Xtra provide this by default. (You usually have to order it.) Without this option, your computer's internet address will change frequently, so nobody will be able to work out how to connect to it.

That said, GameSpy Arcade should be able to work around this, but I'm not an expert in internet networking protocols to be certain if it can handle all possible cases. If you can get a Static IP Address for free, it's probably worth getting one to see if that helps.

(Note that GameSpy Arcade will need its own ports opened too. I don't think our FAQ covers those.)
