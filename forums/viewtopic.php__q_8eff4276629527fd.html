by Geometrian on Tue May 08, 2007 1:47 pm

Well, I don't know of any way to start in a specific place. I would see if anything similar happens again.
If there are about 5 places to start in the desert, and he started 5 times behind you that's 1/(5^5) which is 1 chance in 3125. Very unlikely.
However, if there are 200 people who play multiplayer, and this the only time this has happened in several years, and they play every day, the odds are actually quite good that someone will be spawned behind 5 times in a row. Of course, the probability that this would happen to you is also slim, but it must probably happen to someone, right?

The upshot of this is, your opponent was probably not cheating. I myself have spawned behind the same person 3 times in a row by chance, killing them each time. My internet connection is bad, and I don't play much, so if you play more, this could possibly happen to you as well.
