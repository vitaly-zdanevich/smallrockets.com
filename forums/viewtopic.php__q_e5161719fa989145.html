by vonRedfire on Mon Sep 27, 2004 6:25 pm

Mission 12 can be very frustrating when that loud yellow jacket fighter comes and puts a very big sting on you. I assume that he's the biggest problem. There are a couple of ways to beat him depending on your plane. The easiest one is with the tri-plane. Start off by taking out the three attacking Junkers then swing off to the left and head for the big line of tanks. Of course you are attacked by some junkers along the way. You can make your life easier by taking out the AA with yiour guns at a safe distance. When the 2 rocket bearing junkers come just stay very low and let them fly over you, (this works great with all the rocket bearing planes) then shoot those flyboys down before they can hurt a flea. Proceed to the next base with all the buildings. Here your main enemy is the big bomber with the deadly guns. Make sure you have room to duck low under his bullet barrage and then come around and get him before he can turn on you again. After that 3 rocket bearing planes come after you and you stay low again finishing them off the same as the others. Now you have to get some altitude and head back toward the base where you knocked off the tanks. Fly back and forth at the edge of the combat area by the mountain ridge and lay in wait for the yellow peril to come. When he does swoop down from behind him and give him what for. Ease up on your throttle so that when he turns to the left to come around at you, you can turn with him and continue to riddle him with bullets. This may take a couple of trys to get the hang of it but believe me he's dead meat. Lastly you proceed to the last base and knock off the other tanks and fighters.
Sorry this is so long winded but this is a very long mission. Once you get the hang of this then you can try and beat him with the Junkers which is more difficult and you need to modify your technique to utilize the Junkers superior speed but weaker fire power. I do it the same up to the ambush part which the Junkers can't pull off. With the Junkers I basically rope a dope him by flying away then coming back at him shooting and ducking then flying away again before he can hit me. It takes longer as yiou can't hit him that many times before you have to duck and run. If you get to close you are dead meat.
Well, that's it. Good luck old boy.
