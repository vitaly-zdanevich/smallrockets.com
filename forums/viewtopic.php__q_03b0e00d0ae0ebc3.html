The number of times I have had the feeling that I am the only moron flying a standard-issue plane is testimony to our plight.

Perception of cheating is a tricky thing, especially when imperceptible or non-uniform lag affect how many bullets it takes to shoot down another plane. There have been a few times when the nagging feeling that I am being played for a chump by some pilots, even dinosaurs, has taken over a game as well as my joy. When I have just pummeled some other plane and they get two-bullets into my plane and I go down in flames, it makes me want to scream out..."You F'ing cheater! No way did I not kill you and no way could you have killed me with that measly hit!”

Truth be known, the other player may have put 50 machine gun bullets into my hide during a lag, then when the server or the connection caught up, I only felt (heard) the last two, and boom, I explode.

That is why I don't and I won't yell cheater unless I see an invincible plane or a plane that kills EVERYONE with one or two bullets (PING is the latest). I can't stand all the cheat-sniping in the chat room. Too often the accusations are dead wrong. I have stood up for a bunch of people who were called cheaters over the past 2 plus years. Accusation is a fine line that shouldn’t be crossed without VERY clear evidence. I am proud to say I have been called a cheater many many times. Prouder still to say that I have never cheated.

Fifteen months back, and just for fun, I started coming in as different pilots under different login names. I did this for about three weeks. As KPAX, I was probably in the top 10 back then and won many games, sometimes pretty quickly. I was an early zoom-and-boomer and people had not caught on yet. It’s a different story now and I am not much competition anymore.

I came in as different people because I wanted anonymity. Why?

1. It afforded the ability to come in and wax people who were getting a little big for their britches, people like Marine, etc. They would have to reassess their skills if some no-name came in a beat them handily. It quieted things down a bit.
2. Anonymity was also desirable because when you become a factor (skill wise) aces start paying attention and learn your habits. When you come in as someone else, they have to play you straight up. They have to figure you out all over again. Of course I would only do this for a few games, then I’d tell the friend (ace) that had been perplexed by my newbie abilities, just who I was. We’d have a laugh and that was that. More often then not, the other player would guess who I was anyway. These guys are good.
3. It afforded me the opportunity to continue playing RAS after I had made a fool of myself in the chat room as KPAX.

Clearly I am no Top 10 flyer now. I don’t fly but a few games a month. The squabbling, the drunks, the busters, and the cheaters have completely taken the wind out of my sails. That and every stick I buy fails within the first month. I cant make a pickup without crashing, let alone fly through a tunnel.

Anyway, back to cheating: Literally every time I flew under a false name, LB accused me of being "too good" to be playing honestly and she hounded me and “busted” me repeatedly. The first time she did it, I was appalled. I realized that all those other people she was calling a cheat, probably weren't cheaters either. I decided right then that I would not ever call anyone a cheater again unless I had NO doubt that the player was indeed invincible or way-over-the-top lethal.

I sent her an email and told her that some of the players she had been accusing and abusing were me; someone she had, then-to-fore, considered a good flying buddy. I told her she should reassess her ability to determine who is and who isn’t a cheater. There were games when she had three or four people convinced I was cheating. They were all screaming for me to get out of the game…seasoned players who should have known better.

When she called me (KPAX) a cheater, I lost it. I started cussing and berating her for her behavior. I told her, in the presence of five or six other players in the game, to "Eat shit," then corrected myself and said, "No. Eat wet shit!" That put KPAX on the world-wide web as an ASSHOLE for most of a year.

In retrospect, I am ashamed of my behavior but I illustrate it here to make a point. The good feelings we all had at RAS are in serious jeopardy. LB and I talked it out later, a year later, and she took me off the assholes list. I think LB’s heart is in the right place. I am not so sure about her judgment. But of course, who am I to judge? I have not made it my calling to keep RAS clear of cheaters. I don’t look for cheating as closely as she might. I am no expert.

I think she would have more fun and serve the game better if she would only punkbust cheaters who have been identified by at least five or six ACES other than herself.

Anyway, like XRAY, I take great pleasure in overcoming a cheater, even if the cheat being used is a fabrication of my own paranoid dementia. Sometimes, when I am the dominant player and there is an invincible player in the game, I pretend the cheater is a computer drone designed to make the game tougher. My game becomes win the game without getting killed by the drone, and harass the drone as much as possible and still win the game. You gotta have fun somehow.

What troubles me most is that blatant cheaters remind me of the possibility of cheating shrewdly. Shrewd cheaters are smart enough to cheat just enough to win but not enough to get caught. They don’t make themselves invincible; they make themselves imperceptibly tougher or more lethal...an edge that they can use both mentally and within the virtual/physical realm of the airspace. They can even justify such cheating as legitimate; claiming that they are aces and entitled to a bit of an edge against all those young guns.

If you think that ain’t happening in NASCAR, baseball, politics, and business, then you have your head in the sand. How does it go? “Age and guile beat youth and innocence every time.”

Truly there have been times when I have been tempted to "add a little something to my plane." I can also say in good conscience, that I never have. I feel sorry for people who can’t say that (vonred excluded as it was truly experimental and he came clean immediately). Hell, I feel sorry for men who cheat on their wives...even if they are getting something extra...they are losing out big time.

I confess that I have had these thoughts about certain Aces. Is it true? Does it happen? Hell, how would I ever know? All I know is that I want that edge sometimes but I choose not to lose my integrity over winning or losing a kids' game. I just wish I didn’t have to wonder...but I do.

As I am in my third year of RAS, occasionally I step in something mighty slippery left over from the dinosaurs...and that cheat mentality comes over me. No one likes to be conned and that is why those thoughts slip in once in a while.

Bottom line of all these ramblings is that I think we should keep our mouths shut. I don’t think public forums like SmallRockets – RAS are appropriate venues to blast people.

If you have something to say, say it to the person in private chat or get their email, as LB and I did, and work it out on the side. She leaves me alone. I leave her alone. We haven’t played a complete game together in over 15 months. We seem to be getting along just fine. When she shows up, I leave. Pretty simple. Her game is not my game. That's all. Whose to say what is right and wrong. I can only speak for my own experience.

I have other things to do than complain. After all, we share the net. RAS is public and you aren’t always gonna like what the public has to offer. Different strokes and all that. LB is on my buddy list. I am glad she is my buddy. I need all the friends I can get.

TC

KPAX
