by fzredbaron on Sun Jun 12, 2005 7:51 pm

Jonathan....

First off, I want to tell you how proud I am to be a small part of the Small Rockets Team. It was an HONOR when you selected me to be your Test Team Leader for RAS2..... and it was doubled when, together, you and I created and collaborated on what we both believe is the most functional and customizable flying game ever conceived or made.... RAS PRO.

What people don't know, but WILL now... is that this UPGRADE could have very easily been sold for some money and people would have paid it gladly to have what PRO offers them. It was YOU that told me "I want to make it FREE as a thank you for everyone's support and patience on RAS2". Even being conservative, this generous gesture on your part cost you several thousand dollars (pounds).

Additionally, as you and I BOTH know, we originally wanted to design a few simple things that would make the tournament run a bit smoother. You had NO INTENT on working on this project for OVER A MONTH! And we are talking about, conservatively, over 200 HOURS. WHY? Because YOU made the committment that if we were going to do PRO, we were going to make it FIRST CLASS. You even added things like 4 levels of difficulty for the regular game just to make it even better.

As RAS2 looms over our horizon, I stand today PROUD of my association with you... PROUD of what we have created... and PROUD to be your friend.

Among all the "What's In It For Me" people that cloud the world, you are absolutely the most unselfish and dedicated person that I have ever had the pleasure to meet.

I am a better person for having known you. Let's let PRO speak for us. The Team is ready for our RAS2 assignment down the way and you and I KNOW that PRO is just the tip of the iceberg for what is planned for RAS2.

Cheers my friend! Cheers!

David
aka FZRedBaron
