(Hoping this is read for game design feedback)

I purchased this game recently and have found it to pretty fun, especially in mult-player mode. But, like many others who have posted before me, I too think it could be a lot better with some modifications. It does have a lot of things going for it. I like the graphics and sound. The controls are well balanced; fun and not too easy.


What's wrong with MAD?

1. The AI is weak. Yeah, it's pretty easy to beat in 1-player mode. I think other things need fixed first.

2. Researching "Commercial" makes cash too fast. This creates a big problem with game balance. If you build a station with two research labs both researching commercial, and you repeat this for each of your territiories, you will soon have so much cash that you can't spend it fast enough. By the time I start to build the last station, I was saying to myself "What's the point?" I had almost 100,000 millions and the game had just started. It feels like a cheat.

3. The ECM weapons are good except one. Contrary to what some other people have said, I have found that you cannot rely on just missle research. I found that the Wolfbite Missiles and the Vaporizer to be quite effective and a lot of fun to operate. (Kinda like an updated "Missile Command" game) In fact, the defences may be too effective. In the 2-player games I've played, I've found that once your opponent has a good defence, it's almost impossible to eliminate any of their territories. The game ends up being a race to get the Tesla Gun, and whoever gets it first pretty much wins the game.
With the Tesla you can disable all your opponents space stations. And with no defence, you're toast, err... burnt toast! I like the idea of the Tesla, but it should be a one shot weapon like the Doomsday. That would at least give the other guy half a chance.

4. MAD needs factories. Space station modules are launched too quick. I think it would be better if you had to "build" a module at a factory before launching it. (You would buy the factory just like a launch pad) You would have to wait a short time for a small moduleto be built before launching it. A large or complex module would take much more time. And it would be fun wiating for the factory to finish that shiny new Vaporizer. Also, Nuclear Missiles could be built at a Missile Factory and stored as inventory before use. That way you would have to build the number of missiles you needed before an attack.

5. The Doomsday Device is kind of a letdown. After using the doomsday device, I want to see more than a screen showing that the game is over. I would like to see some animation. (half the world getting destroyed or something) It has to be impressive, otherwise it's a letdown after all that time researching.

Well, that's my MAD wish list. Can anyone tell us what are the plans for support for this game? I realize there are other projects and only so many people. I think this game has winning potential, but it needs some help.

-Kevin.
