FORUM FAQ: If you read nothing else, read this one.

Posted: Thu Jul 05, 2007 4:17 am
by stimarco
Q. Can I post ads, spam or anything else in a similar vein?

A. No. If you do, I will report your abuse to your ISP and/or ban your IP Address(es). No exceptions.


Q. What are these forums for?

A. These forums are for customer support and discussion of Small Rockets' games, both current and forthcoming. Nothing else.


Q. Can I swear and curse?

A. Presumably, but not here. I appreciate that one man's curse is another man's mild salutation, but please do be careful when posting. Not everyone reading these forums is from your home country. These forums are read by people from 6 to 60 or even older, so write accordingly.


Q. What about freedom of speech?

A. What about it? This is a privately owned website, not a public park. Think of it as a private living room, not a public space, and we'll get along swimmingly.


Q. Who's who?

A. I am Sean Baggaley, your benevolent dictator for this evening. The boss is Jon Small and he owns and runs Small Rockets. He also designs and writes the games, runs our decreasingly reliable server, and occasionally blesses these very forums with his presence.

We also have a number of long-standing forum members who have been playing our games -- usually the "Red Ace..." series -- for many years and know far more about its quirks and foibles than yours truly.


Q. Is Small Rockets an English company?

A. Yes, it was originally founded in Guildford, in southern England. In 2005, Jon decided to relocate to the United States with his girlfriend (now his wife), but Small Rockets remains registered as a UK Limited Company.


Q. What happened to all the other folks?

A. Small Rockets used to employ about a dozen programmers and artists. This changed in 2004 after Jon decided to make it a virtual company instead. Small Rockets still has other people working for it, but these folks now work as freelancers or contractors.


Q. Any new games coming out?

A. Absolutely. And not just in the "Red Ace" series either.
