Hi Jim - thanks for the response! We're a little bit limited in this respect as the mission editor doesn't support the kind of randomness you're talking about (for example, varying when and where enemies appear). However, you do get a significant amount of variety which emerges from the game's AI (ie. the interactions between the other planes) - you should find that, although the ultimate goal is always the same, missions frequently play out quite differently each you try them.

By the way, have you played any of the hard missions yet? The challenges are often quite a bit more complex than in the easy ones....

Rob
