Re: Problems with Hot Chix'76 and Art is Dead

by stimarco on Fri Aug 20, 2004 11:26 am

Pablo wrote:this games just dont want to run i have winxp. i think its because of it. are there any patches to fix it?


I don't know about "Art Is Dead", but I do know that the "Hot Chix", "Kayak" and "Jetboat" series are lost causes: we simply cannot get them to run on XP without a _major_ rewrite, and, since they weren't developed in-house, it's extremely unlikely they'll ever be made available for XP users.

"Art Is Dead" was developed in-house as an experiment, but failed to sell in any great numbers, so this may be why it hasn't been updated. It's the only game we have that was designed for a light gun and it's possible that, as nobody seems to make these for PCs any more, it was decided to discontinue support for newer operating systems.

We are working on new games as the existing catalogue is certainly starting to show its age. I think a number of older titles will be removed from sale in the next big website update.

(We spent a couple of years working on big budget titles for other publishers, hence the hiatus.)
