Re: Is Smallrockets dead?

by stimarco on Thu Aug 26, 2004 3:34 am

xray wrote:No answers to phone calls,number has changed,dont return emails....Is Smallrockets dead?


Contrary to a mis-informed article in "Develop" magazine (and others), Small Rockets is NOT dead. We have been 'ill', but we're better now and preparing to kick some serious arse.

We have drastically changed how we do business though. Like most developers, we used to have a big office and everyone based in the same place.

Over the last year or so, we've made the transition from traditional business to "virtual" business. Instead of keeping everyone in-house and requiring long, needless commutes, all our staff are now based at home. Everyone is accessible using an instant messaging app (I use Trillian Pro, but most have AIM or similar), so communications are just as good. And like all good Britons, we all have mobile phones, for those very few occasions when the internet is having an 'off' day.

Support is handled *entirely* online through our new web-based messaging system. This replaced the old support email address which was killed off by spam. (By the end, it was receiving over ten *thousand* spams every day. The server just couldn't cope, our bandwidth was being massacred and our response rate was terrible.) That address is no longer valid.

Small Rockets is now basically an "indie" developer and has gone back to its roots. We're 100% focused on downloadable games now, so expect more. We're developing a sequel to Red Ace Squadron now.

Regards,

--
Small Rockets Support.

by stimarco on Sun Apr 03, 2005 7:51 am

Falling Guy wrote:u missed easter.


We haven't given an official release date because we want to release the game only when it's ready.

Exactly how long it takes depends on a number of factors, some of them personal, since only one programmer is working on it. (Jonathan, in case you're wondering.) However, I believe the current hope is to have at least a beta released sometime in summer this year, with the final release sometime around Christmas. We'd have preferred it to be released earlier, but we also have to maintain and support our existing games, so website tweaks, bug reports on older games that need to be investigated, etc. all need to be taken care of.

We're building RAS 2 with a much smaller team -- one programmer, one artist -- than was used on RAS 1, so even a bout of the 'flu will have a delaying effect.

But it's coming, folks! It _is_ coming. Just not today.

We'll keep you posted.
