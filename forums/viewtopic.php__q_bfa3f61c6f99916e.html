by Spike on Mon Jan 26, 2004 8:30 pm

I had a similar problem through Real Arcade. I think for $5 a month I got a free game then if I wanted any more I could get a discount. I got the Red Ace free but after that they had no games in which I was interested, also I could not even get on line to play. I think you are better off to just bite the bullet and do what I did, delete it then buy your version through Small Rockets.
