It may do, but I don't think it's possible to just install it over DirectX 9. DirectX goes deep into the heart of Windows and Microsoft don't make it easy to remove or roll back.

From the sound of things, I suspect Windows itself may need reinstalling as it seems either the driver or DirectX itself have failed to register themselves correctly. Trust me, it's a lot less pain doing it this way than tinkering around with drivers. Drivers, by their very nature, live at a very low level in Windows and getting something wrong here can easily make Windows stop working entirely.

I tend to reinstall any operating system I use about once or twice a year as a rule; it's a good way to de-clutter the system. (Don't forget to backup your important data first!)
