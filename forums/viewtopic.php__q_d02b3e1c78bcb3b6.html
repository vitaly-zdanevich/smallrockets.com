Having just completed this mission, I can tell you the secret. Take the Triplane, first of all, as you can't win with the Junkers.

The Triplane has an amazing climb rate. So amazing that no other plane on the board can catch you, ever. The key to this scenario is to climb whenever you feel the heat- you can handle most of the opposition in a regular turning match, but "Captain Ahab", the yellow plane, will give you a lot of trouble.

Climb when your radar shows three or even two bad guys coming after you, and keep climbing until the image of your plane and radar shakes.
Then level off a bit, swing around and go for a steep dive on your opponents. They will try to fire, but will find it almost impossible to get anywhere near you. Blast one of them, but don't risk too much- climb back up, and repeat until done.

The only enemies that will give you trouble in this are the yellow plane which might be able to give you a burst while you dive on him, and of all things, the Handley-Page bomber! They can climb fairly steep and spray the heck out of you with their machineguns.

With this stategy, you'll have more to fear from AA as you bomb targets than from any fighters.

For those still stuck on the last mission before the Secret Mission, this might help. Of course, wipe out the three AckAck guns on top of the mountain or your squadron will be hit hard. Then time your attacks on the other planes so that when the last three come over the mountain just circle and "toy" with them for a while...don't shoot them down. Your men will come and attack them. Hopefully, your men won't shoot down all the planes before the Yellow plane comes out. Delay until the Yellow plane and his wingmen come out. Shoot down the wingmen and you might have four or five of your planes to attack The Yellow Horde. Fly high so your men can get shots at the plane as it tries to attack you. A fun thing to do, although it's not pure fighting, is to land behind a structure or Ack Ack gun and wait. The yellow plan flys low and crashes. It's really unfair the way the game is set up...you arrive shotup and no wingmen and he arrives fresh with extended endurance, wingmen and manuverability.
