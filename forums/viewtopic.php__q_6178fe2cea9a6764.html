Here's a solution for the mission from one of our regular players:

It’s a tricky one. You have to fight off the 3 Allied planes that attack you just after takeoff. Then proceed to the enemy bases and start bombing the tanks and base areas. When 3 Allied planes appear (one of them a bomber) you must kill the bomber first or its "firehose" machine gun will wipe you out in short order. When they appear stay away from the edge of the playing area (where they appear from) and keep near the center of the playing field. This gives you enough time to gain height and get a good angle of attack on the bomber i.e. above and behind or from the side.....forget head-on attacks. That ought to do it. When you get to the same mission in master mode. You have to take out the AA guns, which are placed on top of the large "volcano" like rock in the center of the mission map. These kill many of your wingmen who appear later in the game to save you and must be taken out first or you will be overwhelmed.
p.s.
Only a rare few have been able to complete the final mission (master mode) flying the Junker. The combination of the Neiuport and vast swarms of Allied planes make it extremely difficult...try it!
