VISTA COMPATIBILITY (Read this BEFORE contacting SR Support)

by stimarco on Sun Apr 08, 2007 3:27 pm

The current situation:

We do NOT officially support our games Windows Vista at present. This is why there is no mention of Vista in our "minimum requirements" pages.

There are a number of reasons for this, not the least of which is that we've had some major headaches just getting Vista to install on our computers. (Well, Jon's had problems. My ancient Athlon PC is old enough to have support for its hardware included in Vista's supplied drivers. My other PC is an iMac.)

Vista has some major architectural changes which will make converting some of Small Rockets' games difficult. Basically, Jon has to decide which games will get the treatment and also work out when to do the work.

Jon is already working hard on RAS 2 and there is nobody else to handle any additional work, so RAS 2 must either be delayed, (again!) or we have to just put up with a flood of support requests. The latter gives a poor impression of our customer service, while delaying RAS 2 makes SR look like a dead company. It's not an easy choice to make.

*

That said, it is possible to get most of our games to run under Vista if you follow these steps:

1. Install the game in "Compatibility Mode".
- Vista will usually offer to do this for you after the installer bails out on the first attempt.

2. Disable the "Aero" theme.
- you can always switch it back on again after playing the game.

3. Ensure you have "Administrator" rights.
- without this, you'll have problems unlocking our games, although the game should run normally aside from this one issue.


As long as you are aware of all three points above, you should be able to run most of our games without much stress.

I should point out that Vista does have one advantage over XP: it seems to be more compatible with the games in our "Free Stuff" area.
