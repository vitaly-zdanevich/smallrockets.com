Highest possible accuracy percentage for a single mission

by Snoopy on Fri May 18, 2007 8:46 am

I was thinking about bombs too. In Central4 you're limited by your 15 missiles. It's probably possible to get more than 6000% accuracy on Central5 Normal Mode ... if you fire ONE round only and then use bombs on everything ... all enemy planes, buildings, tanks, and AA guns. You can get more bombs in Normal Mode because you can replenish. And you don't have to worry about missing because it won't ever count as a round fired. Even though there are more targets in Master Mode, you only get 30 bombs. You can find more than 30 targets in Normal Mode. But to get the highest accuracy percentage possible, you would need to bomb every enemy plane before they crash or get shot down by a comrade. Bombing your comrades would be an option. Apparently, bombing oil wells and stuff doesn't help ... just radar targets (including AA guns). Apparently, some AA guns are worth more than others. The AA guns in the Allied1 canyon seem to worth a lot. So I'm not completely sure which non-secret mission could give you the highest accuracy percentage, but I'm pretty sure that it's possible to get higher than 6000% (the Central4 max).
