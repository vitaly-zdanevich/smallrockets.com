HQRAS Game TIPS & STRATEGIES

by fzredbaron on Sun Jan 22, 2006 11:41 am

DON'T CONFUSE "LAG" AS "CHEATING"

We decided to put this newest topic at the top, since several NEW PILOTS are unaware of what LAG is and confuse it with CHEATING. First off, rest easy, the SERVER controls the game. ANYONE who attempts to change any files to do with munitions and plane dynamics will NEVER get into another PRO SERVER! Just NOT going to happen. What IS happening, however, is LAG.

LAG occurs when the HOST SERVER cannot process the incoming data from the PLAYER MACHINES at a FAST ENOUGH LEVEL where gameplay is NORMAL. This can be further damaged depending on the ISP that the HOST SERVER is using. T-1 thru T-3, Cable and DSL are the fastest ISP's that HOST SERVERS can utilize. If you are trying to HOST with DIAL-UP.... forget it.

Some of the TOP SYMPTOMS of LAG are:

1. Twirling (Dancing) Planes

2. Bullets Coming Out Of Other Places Than The Nose

3. Having To Shoot Someone More Than Normal To Kill Them (Rockets Or Bullets)

4. Flying And All Of The Sudden Dieing Without Seemingly Being Shot

5. Planes Disappearing And Reappearing

In addition to these factors.... connecting through GAMESPY also adds to the problem. Unlike a PEER to PEER connection.... the HOST SERVERS show in GAMESPY. When a connection is made THROUGH the GAMESPY LOBBY, you are actually connecting through a MEDIAN DEVICE (GameSpy). So, you can see quickly that if the HOST SERVER does not have a TOP OF THE LINE computer processor, graphics card, mega ram and fast internet connection speed.... LAG is going to happen. Even with the top of the line servers that are available in the GameSpy lobby, because of the PLAYERS and their own PERSONAL SPEEDS... they can LAG even the BEST of the HOST SERVERS. DIAL-UP PLAYERS really KILL any HOST SERVER.

Last, but not least, INTERNET TRAFFIC during a given time period world wide also is a BIG LAG PRODUCER. Certain times of the day, HOST SERVERS really kick butt.... other times of the day..... they STINK.

The best suggestion to REDUCE the LAG problem (I say REDUCE because it is NEVER going to be eliminated).... is to RESTRICT the number of players allowed to 6 for FAST MACHINES and 4 for SLOWER MACHINES. You may find that you have to reduce this number even LOWER for optimum results.

Playing in YOUR OWN SERVER can also drag down the server. If you wish to PLAY in your own server.... DON'T CONNECT THROUGH GAMESPY! Instead, start your game from your desktop, then go to Multi-Player and connect by "LAN". This connects you DIRECTLY to your OWN COMPUTER without going through GameSpy.

Hope this helps you newer guys to understand how LAG plays a vital role in RAS game play.

The GOOD NEWS is.... that the NEW TECHNOLOGY that will be implemented in RAS2 should eliminate MOST of the LAG PROBLEMS.

FLIGHT PATHS/KILL ZONES

Most people don't realize that when you play multi-player, that the only thing that is really universal on all systems is the PLANES and the WEAPONS. The landscape, buildings, trees, etc. are only unique to your own system as far as the game is concerned. What this means is that when YOU destroy a tree or a building or a tower on YOUR MAP, it is only destroyed on YOUR COMPUTER. Everyone else still sees the existing object because they are playing using their own layout. Most of the pros will CLEAR a killing area as they fly during a game. By eliminating trees, buildings and towers (especially around pick-ups), it makes for a CLEARER flight path and also a CLEARER "Kill Zone". That is why, if you are observant of others, you will see them shoot at objects during flight. That is also why you will see bullets and rockets coming out of trees or buildings... because they have CLEARED them on their layout and you see them coming through objects that you have not destroyed. So, Clearing your flight path will definitely put you on a more level playing field with some of the ones who already know this.

RADAR (IF ALLOWED)

This is not emphasized enough in my opinion. Of course, with PRO it will depend on whether RADAR, in some form, is even used. Most pros, keep one eye on the aerial combat they are in and the other eye on the radar. When I am in a multi-plane aerial combat, if I see someone coming up on my six, I will break off from the rat pack and engage the single plane coming into the group. Several of the old pros KNOW that flying close to the ground (STEALTH MODE) makes them invisible in the others RADAR. This is especially important in games where there are multiple players. You can SNEAK up on the engaged group and take out one or two before you get nailed yourself. Again, keep in mind, this will depend on the HOST SETTINGS of the PRO SERVER that you are in.

STAY LOADED (IF ALLOWED)

Again, this will depend on the settings of the HOST PRO SERVER. I can't say enough how important it is to NOT run yourself dry of ROCKETS or BOOST (if allowed). Even if it means that you have to evade someone rather than taking them on... even by keeping just a couple of rockets or 20% boost before you refill, that will give you a superior edge if needed. Unless you are playing my favorite type of game GUNS ONLY... if you are UNLOADED you are DEAD MEAT!

SMOKING = DEATH

If you see any plane that is damaged (SMOKING)... that is the WEAKEST LINK in the battle. Once a plane is damaged, it can be taken out quickly with guns (depending on your plane of choice... determines HOW QUICKLY) or another rocket shot. As has been pointed out already... in a battle... if you have damaged the opponent it is obvious where they are headed, if allowed, the HEALTH PICK-UP. If you have been killed, when you RESPAWN head for the HEALTH and take them out. As most of you have already gathered... picking up HEALTH actually can work against you in battle. Many will tell you in an instant that when you utilize HEALTH it really doesn't give you FULL STRENGTH even though your meter will show full... you will take damage at an accelerated rate. Some will not utilize the HEALTH pick-ups at all and continue in battle until they are killed to REGAIN their regular health strength.

LEAD YOUR TARGET

The Pros in RAS have been playing the game since 2001. They know this and now you will too. ROCKETS tend to LOFT or RISE when deployed. This means that you should aim a bit LOW on your target. In addition, you should LEAD your target with GUNS and ROCKETS by a good plane's length. If you shoot AT the target, your munitions will hit DEAD AIR. BOOST and SHOOT is a must to learn. By BOOSTING and SHOOTING a ROCKET or BULLETS while BOOSTING... you INCREASE the SPEED of the ROCKETS and/or BULLETS. This can be extremely important in a close match. It just takes a little practice.

SECRET MISSION PRACTICE

If you ever wanted a PERFECT training ground for PRO Multi-Player training, the Secret Mission is the place to go. To UNLOCK all the levels and get to the Secret Mission, simply START your single player game, go to MULTIPLAYER, click on SERVER PASSWORD and then type in (ALL LOWER CASE)

boelcke

This is your perfect training ground for more ADVANCED COMBAT! This is where you can practice LEADING your targets with GUNS and ROCKETS. It will also give you great EXPERIENCE with multiple plane scenarios. Any pro will tell you that playing the MISSIONS MODE COMPLETELY and entering the SECRET MISSION is the perfect TRAINING GROUND for RAS PRO and the upcoming RAS2. This is a great place when you want to LEARN at your OWN PACE before engaging REAL LIVE OPPONENTS. Be SURE to try this on ALL LEVELS including the ACE difficulty level.

IMMELMAN LOOP

Any pilot worth his salt will tell you that one of the most critical things a pilot must do in order to be successful in aerial combat is the Immelman Loop. Check out this site. It SHOWS you how to do this.

http://www.furball.warbirdsiii.com/krod ... elman.html

AIRSPEED

Being able to ADJUST your AIRSPEED during combat is critical. By lowering your airspeed in turns or loops, you can actually SHARPEN your turn and overtake your opponent. Depending on your aircraft, you will need to play with this a bit and find out what you can and can't do successfully with different aircraft.

AIRBRAKES

Yes... RAS PRO aircraft DO have airbrakes. This is the technique and how to do it when you are being pursued by another aircraft. Reduce your AIRSPEED to ZERO. If they are close on your tail, they will fly in front of you and you can take them out. Also, if you are boosting and someone is boosting after you, reduce your airspeed while boosting to zero... when you stop boosting they will shoot past you because you will come to a slow stop.

DEAD KILLS

Dating back to World War I, combat pilots have always had one singular mission... to kill their enemy before their enemy kills them. However, there are countless documented accounts of pilots taking out enemy positions even after they, themselves, have been injured or damaged where flight is no longer possible. The Japanese, in World War II, of course were the masters of dead kills. And just like REAL WAR, this can also be achieved in RAS PRO.

Once you have been killed, depending on the plane, you can stay in the air from 10 seconds (Junker) to over 3 minutes (Airco). It works better with a joystick than a mouse. The technique is simple, when you are killed, pull back on your stick (or mouse) and head straight up. If you have some boost left... great! When you get to the ceiling (and as you are looking at your radar, if it is an option, at the targets available), you simply lower your nose and head for the action. Obviously, having ROCKETS is critical. Most of the pros can take out at least one or two depending on the amount of aircraft still in aerial combat. Most of the regulars also know... that if you kill an experienced pilot... go HIGH immediately. Once they start down, they can't go back up and thus you avoid being a DEAD KILL. If I had a dollar for every time I have been killed myself because I forgot my own rule, I would be a richer man today. Remember, this is a GAME but it is AERIAL COMBAT...... not checkers.

ROCKET TECHNIQUES

Most of the pros can hit a target of their choosing from great distances... because they know from experience, how long it will take their rocket(s) to hit a designated impact point from a certain distance (normal or boosted). They AIM for WHERE they think the target will be when the rocket(s) arrive. They also employ what is called a VOLLEY or a SPREAD. Like a submarine commander, most pros will shoot a spread of several rockets at a designated impact point, which greatly increases the probability of a hit or kill. With a little practice, by yourself, you can practice different shots from different distances and can quickly improve your game and kill ratio. Of course, these techniques are used for long distance shooting... close in aerial combat is just a matter of leading your target.

VIEW KEYS

The VIEW KEYS are the ARROW KEYS. If you have one of the programmable joysticks, you can program your TOP HAT to do these functions. The advantage of the VIEW KEYS can be impressive. Although it may take some getting use to, it will give you a better idea of what the person you are in aerial combat with is doing and intends to do.

DON'T BE PREDICTABLE

For the seasoned pro, it won't take them long to learn a pilot's tendencies. Like any HABIT, people get comfortable doing things the same way. This can be DANGEROUS against a pro. Once they identify what your TENDENCIES are in aerial combat, it gives them a tremendous advantage. My suggestion to you is to CHANGE UP from time to time what you do in combat.

DON'T PANIC

Every pilot in the RAS community had to start out at the bottom. You are ALWAYS going to come across and compete against SOMEONE, who on a given day, will be better than you. So... DON'T PANIC... remember that by playing the BETTER PLAYERS your OWN GAME PLAY will improve DRAMATICALLY along the way. LEARN from the pros. WATCH what they DO. Have a question? ASK THEM. The benefit of this game is NOT to be an ACE... but to be COMPETITIVE, HAVE FUN and MAKE FRIENDS!

BIGGER IS NOT BETTER

The TYPICAL newbie and rookie mistake when they first start playing MULTI-PLAYER.... is they find themselves getting their ass kicked by pilots who are veterans.... and immediately think... "I NEED A BIGGER PLANE". Well.... unlike the quote from Jaws where Roy says "WE NEED A BIGGER BOAT".... that would have worked well for them... but NOT in RAS. Unless everyone is playing a ALL BOMBER GAME (which is tons of fun), selecting a BOMBER against a Junker, Airco, Halber etc., is SHEER SUICIDE. Although the BOMBERS have great guns.... they are like trying to park a 18 WHEELER in the space for a Volkswagen..... a BITCH. You will get cut down SO FAST. The bomber is NO MATCH for a smaller, FASTER PLANE.
