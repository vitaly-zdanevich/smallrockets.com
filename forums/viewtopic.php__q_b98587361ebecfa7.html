by vonRedfire on Sun Jan 29, 2006 7:30 am

Actually, as an avid MOTS player in the past, I can assure you that there was boost. It was one of the "cheats" that was unlocked after you completed the game. I use to use it to chase afetr the Red Baron in the earlier missions where he just did a fly by but was not in the battle.

BTW, RBR451, were you aware that you could win some of the intense missions between 21 and 25 by just bobbing and weaving around the aquaduct pillars. I use to have fun trying to win these missions without ever firing a shot. You could even defeat the Red Baron this way.

As for boost in the multplaer RAS, you may want to check your joystick routing in the red ace configuaration file. I believe it is normally routed to the number 3 trigger, but this may not be good for your stick. It can be rerouted to a different part of your joy stick by just changing the number and saving it. Honestly you don't want to play RAS Pro multiplayer without boost as it has become a prime tool of many fliers. If I can help you out further with any of this(if I have in fact been any help at all)feel free to email me.

Good luck.
