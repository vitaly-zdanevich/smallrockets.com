Dash234 wrote:is RASII being pushed to next year?

I haven't heard specific dates from Jon, but a release towards the end of 2007 does look slim. Early 2008 seems more likely to me. (The Christmas / New Year break would give Jon much-needed time to work on RAS 2, so that may make all the difference.)

One important point is that RAS 2 will require some rewriting of server code too, including the old licence code system and registration database (both of which are major sources of support issues). Even if the game were completed by December this year, the other pieces of the puzzle may still hold it back.

This is an educated guess, not a guarantee folks; please don't take it as gospel!

by stimarco on Thu Oct 18, 2007 11:30 am

Dash234 wrote:are they any new photos of RASII?

Nope. Only those already on the site.

is there like a trailer video?

None I'm aware of. Jon has considered doing one, but he doesn't have any suitable software for that.

how is the gameplay?

"Not a million miles away from that of RAS Pro" would be a good bet.

what new features are going to be displayed in the game?

Aside from the new technologies? "More of the same" seems to be what most of the "Red Ace" fans want, so that's what they're gonna get. It'll still be a WW1 dogfighting game. It'll just look a lot better and have vastly improved networking and configuration features.

how are the new graphics, are they compatible with new line graphics cards?

Graphics will be wa-a-a-ay better than those in RAS Pro. As for which cards will be compatible, I'd say anything from the Intel GMA 950 on upwards. (And possibly older cards too.)

Although the engine will be capable of all the graphical buzzwords, there's little point in including overblown graphics and models as only those with the highest-end computers will ever see it all. (And the game will be download-only, so we can't include gigabytes of content.)
