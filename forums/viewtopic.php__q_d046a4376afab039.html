by stimarco on Wed Oct 06, 2004 2:22 am

Hi Meerups,

Could you post your request to our Support team? It's easier for our support and development teams to track requests there.

As I understand it, if the uninstall-restart-reinstall trick doesn't work, it usually means there's either a subtle driver problem or some form of corruption in Windows' Registry. The former can usually be resolved by updating your audio, video and (if necessary) chipset drivers.

You can download the free Belarc Advisor applet which can generate a full audit of your PC's hardware here: http://www.belarc.com/free_download.html. If you can paste it into your support request, we'd appreciate it.

The only other solution that seems to work is, unfortunately, a fresh reinstallation of Windows. The Support team say they're willing to offer a refund instead if you don't want the hassle.

Our apologies for the inconvenience. We are planning to rewrite the protection system we use, but this probably won't happen until RAS2 is ready for release -- i.e. sometime in the first half of 2005.
