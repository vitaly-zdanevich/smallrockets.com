A Tank Game, please?

by Julian Bury on Mon Mar 25, 2002 12:43 am

Conquerer was a good tank game written for the Acorn Archimedes.
The important thing about it was the three modes of play; Arcade, Attrition and Strategy.

Arcade: One tank. Enemy tanks are approaching in numbers that increase until you die.

Attrition: Race to a target area, clear it and hold it till the timeout.

Strategy: Full scale battle. You have charge of a number of tanks for which you can set GOTO points and which you can jump between to drive or reset points as the circumstances demand.

Small Rockets should seriously consider converting this game, particularly for the choice of play modes.

It was written by Jonathan Griffiths and David Braben. David Braben was also responsible for ELITE which was first written for the BBC Micro.

Perhaps one of them could help with the creation of your version?
