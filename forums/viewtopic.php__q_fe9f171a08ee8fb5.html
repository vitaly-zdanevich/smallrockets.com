How about a tank game please

by Julian Bury on Mon Mar 25, 2002 12:33 am

I remember this game from the Acorn Archimedes :)
Please don't miss the most important point: "There were 3 modes of play Arcade, Attrition and Strategy."
Arcade: You have one tank. Enemy tanks are approaching in numbers that increase until you die.
Attrition: You race to a target area, clear it and hold it till the timeout.
Strategy: In a full scale battle, you have charge of a number of tanks for which you can set goto points and which you can jump between to drive or reset points as the circunstances change.
Small Rockets should seriously consider this game, particularly for the choice of play modes.
It was written by Jonathan Griffiths and David Braben. David Braben was also responsible for ELITE which was first written for the BBC Micro.
Perhaps one of them could help with the creation of your version?
