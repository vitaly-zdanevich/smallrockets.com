Hi Easy. In answer to your question..... the LARGE percentage of the reason would be the server LAG. What YOU see on YOUR MONITOR may not be what THEY SEE on theirs. Of course, as I have already said, LAG can change these factors tremendously.

Trust me.... there is NO SECRET CODE or any of that BS. It is simply the nature of the beast of multi-player. When RAS2 and it's new game engine comes out, you will see a much more accurate and detailed product.
