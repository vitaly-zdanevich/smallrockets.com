Have found a solution to the XP problem

by Michelle Bailey on Wed Feb 13, 2002 3:45 am

I have tried all possible combinations of Compatability mode, nothing works. I originally gave up. I went to my sisters for christmas and took my computer with me which has an IBM G42 monitor i think.......anyway I said to my sister lets use my box while im here as its faster then your HP you got, I said dont worry about moving your monitor I'll plug my box into your monitor nd use yours. For some strange reason i said i might see if Kayak works (dont know why i thought it would) and guess what, the bit where it goes to change the resolution, which is were it always dumped out on me, It didnt this time, the game worked :) i was stoked, so i downloaded the demo for Jet boats 2 to see as well, low and behold jet boat worked, thought merry xmas :).....until i plug it back into my monitor, Didnt work again, would dump out right where it goes to change the resolution or whatever its doing after you click its icon, so i plugged my sisters monitor back in and it worked again, did the old in out of monitors again to make sure.

What i noticed about here monitor compared to what mine was doing (hers is a newer monitor, mine was puchased back in 97-98) Hers when going to change what ever it does to the screen, actually puts the monitor into stand by then back on.

I have tested a few monitors and all the ones that i have tried that sorta go into stand by and back out all play the games with know help from the compatabilty tab, all the ones that dont do the on off of the monitor wont run the games.

We do try where it's feasible to sort out as many of these issues as we can but we'll never be able to sort it all.

It frustrating for us as well . . .
