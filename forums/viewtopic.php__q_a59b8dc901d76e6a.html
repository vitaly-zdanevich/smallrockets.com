Re: how is RAS2 doing

by stimarco on Thu Jul 09, 2009 12:52 pm

Dash234 wrote:are there any new news on RAS2? is jon almost completeing it?
how will the new gameplay be different from the other games?
how many levels will be in the game?>
will there be downloadable mission packs? or dogfight sessions?
will there be a more variety of airplanes?


I'll try to answer your questions in order...

1. RAS 2 will be more of the same, only better, with new levels, (some) new planes, etc. Details are still sketchy as there are budgetary and manpower issues to consider. We don't yet know exactly how much money will be available to pay artists, musicians, etc., for the work that needs doing. Which is why Jon is currently working on fixing the major problems with our current games to make them work on current versions of Windows.

2. I have no idea how many levels will be in the game as this depends entirely on when Jon wants to get it released. Once a release date is known, we'll have a better idea of how many new levels we can create, test and fine-tune in the time available. This is stuff that happens towards the later stages of development and the game isn't there yet.

3. The new game engine is much more amenable to downloadable content and new features, so these are definitely planned. If, when, and how much new stuff will be produced depends entirely on how well RAS 2 sells.

4. It may come as a surprise, but Jon paid an artist to replace all the old RAS graphics for RAS 2 some time ago. Some of this art can be seen in the screenshots pages. Most of the new art has been done, but some work still remains, and there's currently no music or sound for the game either.

*

Compatibility with Windows Vista and Windows 7 (and, for some of our games, even Windows XP SP3), has become our most common support query. We're having to turn down sales because of the compatibility issues, so Jon has been forced to move forward the planned "remakes" of some of our older games so they can continue to be offered for sale. "Tower of The Ancients" is currently up on the blocks having its old engine removed with extreme prejudice for replacement by the shiny, leaner, meaner and more efficient model. This remake process will help Jon iron out any wrinkles in the new engine and fine-tune it.

The completion of RAS 2 is therefore in the queue behind the remake of "Tower of The Ancients" and our original "Red Ace" games. Jon needs to do the older games first as they're known to sell well. ("Tower" was chosen as the first to get the new game engine mainly because it's technically quite challenging to convert. It's the ultimate acid test for the new engine.) There's not much Jon can do to bring RAS 2 forward as there's only him working on all this.

All the really hard work for RAS 2—the new engine and tools—has now been done and is being put through its paces by these remakes to ensure it's as good as it can be for the new game.
--
Sean Baggaley
SR Support.
