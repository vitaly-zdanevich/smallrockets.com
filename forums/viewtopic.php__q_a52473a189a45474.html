Warning! Spoofing and Phishing attacks

Posted: Fri Jun 17, 2005 6:56 am
by jonathan
It's come to our attention, that parties unknown have engaged in spoofing and phishing our customers. We don't know how widespread this is, but we know at least one customer who has received multiple attacks.

Spoofing and phishing involves a criminal sending email, pretending to be a legitimate party (in this case SmallRockets) in order to get the recipient to do something (for example install a virus) or to get sensitive information (such as passwords).

SmallRockets will not send you any emails with an attachment. SmallRockets will also never send you an email asking you to change your password or provide sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card information.

Please do not open any email attachments to a mail that claims to have come from SmallRockets.

All contact between SmallRockets and customers can be handled through our website - partly to try and avoid problems such as this. Please go to the 'contact us' section of the website for more details.

For your safety we always recommend you have the latest version of virus protection software on your machine. Never open attachments on an email if you do not know the other parties identity.

Thank you for your understanding in this situation.

For more information try...
