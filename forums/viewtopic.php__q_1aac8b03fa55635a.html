by Snoopy on Sat May 26, 2007 5:57 pm

Yes. If you start a new pilot, shoot your five missiles in Central1 at non-target buildings and trees, then abort the mission and quit the game. Then, in your Program Files, open the Small Rockets folder, open the Red Ace Squadron folder, and open the Characters file. Look up the new pilot and you should see "shotsontarget=0". If you do the mission again and shoot one missile and hit an official target, you should see that "shotsontarget" is one or three or something ... depending on what kind of target you hit. Best I can figure is that the accuracy formula is:

((shotsontarget + kills) x 100) / rounds fired = accuracy percentage

Editing the Characters file is a good way to gain access to all missions without playing them.

Nice picture. I think I only had 13 following before.
