Development update

by jonathan on Wed Jun 25, 2008 4:16 am

Hi,

I've been very quiet about development. I've been trying to get my head down and get things done.

Well the good news is that good progress is being made. Previously Small Rockets had a collection of technologies, which was used for producing games. One of the biggest pieces of technology we are now unable to use. A lot of the other technology was either in bit rot, not cross platform, or hackery. When I started down this track I wanted to have a foundation that all future SR games could run on. I have to admit I didn't think it was going to take this long, or I'd learn so much along the way. RAS2 was the first game designed to use this new technology layer. The tech has been through multiple revisions to get to this point, including being effectively thrown out. Thankfully and finally it is coming together.

The plan at this point is to finish off the few remaining technology components. Then before finishing off RAS2 to move one or perhaps more (depending on how easy it is) of the existing games over to run on that technology. This will test, the tech as well as producing an updated and improved version of the game moved over.

Anyway I'm not going to predict when RAS2 and this update is going to be released, as I've done that before and been wildly off. All I can say is things are progressing, and RAS2 is going to happen.

Thanks for your patience,

Jonathan
