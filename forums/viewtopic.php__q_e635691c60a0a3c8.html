I keep one very important thing in the forefront of my thinking when playing online; diversity.

Man, are we diverse… so many differences, global, age, all sorts of backgrounds and should I say rearing, and the list goes on. I just thank God, or whoever we are suppose to thank these days for just still being around and having all the online friends to play these games with.

We all actually get to know each other a little better, the community becomes self governing over time… and it is a lot more fun to speak with your friends, get to know their family, and so forth versus just typing on a computer screen.
