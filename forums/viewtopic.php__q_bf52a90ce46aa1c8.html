I really LOVE playing Kayak Extreme. Thanks for a great game! It is the most realistic kayak slalom game that I know of (the other one being Sydney 2000 which has an uncontrollable boat and poor water features).

Just a few questions and further comments:

1. Does anybody know where I can find the high scores for the standard locations and difficulties of other players on the web? If not, please post your best championship time or any other location record for each difficulty setting.

2. The third person view works well, but I wanted to get closer to the action by creating a first person view. I edited the camera.csv file in the ..\Kayak Extreme\Data\Tracks\Alpine\Com directory to read

Camera section
Near camera
CameraDist 0 (this places the camera at the paddler, and not behind)
CameraHeight 0 (this changes the actual height of the camera above the paddlers head)
CameraAim 0.9 (this lets the camera "look up" more, otherwise you keep staring at the nose of the kayak in stead of seeing the gates)
Far camera
CameraDist 2
CameraHeight 1.7
CameraAim 0.75
SideView camera
CameraDist 2
CameraHeight 1.7
CameraAim 0.75

One can edit the file for each location (Training, Alpine, South Africa and Alaska) to change the camera for that specific location.
Any comments on this view or perhaps a better setting for a first person view would be welcomed!

3. Could someone please tell me how to edit/change the placing of the gates on the different levels? (which files, etc.) Perhaps Smallrockets could provide us with a small editor program. If this is too difficult, I wonder if Smallrockets would be able to provide us with a few alternative "track-files" that could be used on the locations. (Especially for the Alpine location - my favourite river. I do appreciate the South African river in the game since I'm from South Africa myself

Thanks again to Smallrockets for an awesome game.
