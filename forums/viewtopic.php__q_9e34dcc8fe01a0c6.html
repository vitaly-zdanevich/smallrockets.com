In theory, Linux could be supported. Unfortunately, we've tried supporting Linux before and lost money on the process. Support is simply a nightmare: there is no unified, cross-distro standard for installers and there simply isn't a market big enough to justify spending any effort on it.

(Linux supporters often claim that it has a decent share of the market, but the high proportion of server installations skews the statistics. Its share of the *desktop* market, which is the only market we care about, is miniscule.)
