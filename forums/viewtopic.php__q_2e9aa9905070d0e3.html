Same price for old games as new.

by Kevin Amedy on Mon Dec 23, 2002 12:49 am

Why does Small Rockets charge the same price for their old games as they do for their newer games.
Example: Hot Chix 'N Gears Stix has 6 women and 3 race tracks. Superchix '76 has 8 women and 4 race tracks. Both cost $14.99.
Example: Jetboat Superchamps has 6 drivers and 3 race tracks. Jetboats Superchamps 2 has 8 drivers and 5 race tracks. Both cost $14.99.
It does not make sence to pay the same price and get less for the older games. Here in Amedrica I am finding the Gobal Star Software versions of your games marked down to as low as $4.99. Why do you charge such a high price for your downloads?

Same price for old games as new.

by Sean Baggaley on Wed Jan 08, 2003 11:28 pm

> Originally From Kevin Amedy at 00:49 on 23-12-2002

> Example: Hot Chix 'N Gears Stix has 6 women and 3 race tracks. Superchix '76 has 8 women and 4 race tracks. Both cost $14.99.
> Example: Jetboat Superchamps has 6 drivers and 3 race tracks. Jetboats Superchamps 2 has 8 drivers and 5 race tracks. Both cost $14.99.
> It does not make sence to pay the same price and get less for the older games. Here in Amedrica I am finding the Gobal Star Software versions of your games marked down to as low as $4.99. Why do you charge such a high price for your downloads?

Mark-downs like that are done by distributors and retailers, not developers. It's a way of clearing out warehouses of old stock to make way for new products. People with older PCs rarely buy games, so this is really a last-resort tactic to milk a bit more cash from impulse buyers who might pick a game out of the bargain bin while queueing up to pay for their expensive copy of ReallyViolentGame 3.

The above clearly doesn't apply if you're a developer with your own distribution and retail channel and a 'warehouse' that takes the form of a couple of hard drives on a web server. Presumably the older games are still selling or SR would have dropped the prices. (There are plenty of people out there with older PCs that won't run the newer games.)

That's my theory anyway. :)
