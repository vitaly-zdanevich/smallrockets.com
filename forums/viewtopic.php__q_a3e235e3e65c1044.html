> I am an AUTHORITY on the topic, as this is my favourite genre of all time.

and you forget one of the best ever games of the genre? saint dragon. in my opinion it's the best shooter ever made... that is before star monkey.

but the question originally posed was not dos games. although MAME isn't DOS, or rather, not just DOS, it's an arcade emulator. and the games emulated tend to be old..

I think it's interesting to see how this genre has developed over the last few decades. when shoot-em-ups were at their simplest, ala Defender, they had the most addictive gameplay anyone could ask for.

over the years, a lot of great games were produced, and in the last few years, a lot of game designers have tried to implement the fun of a 2d shooter with 3d graphics. games on consoles and on PCs have failed. a game called The Reap came close, but it was odd because it was kind of an isometric 3d game.. flying on a 30 degree angle can be odd.

I was just introduced to Star Monkey yesterday morning, and about 4 minutes later I had purchased it.

NOTHING I know of even comes close to it in modern games. it uses 3d graphics perfectly to bring 3d gfx to a classic 2d genre.

why bother with any other games?
I have my doubts as to whether I'll be playing diablo 2 or quake 3 again for a while...

There have been two freeware 3d verticle shooters similar to Star Monkey released this year. Both stick to the tried and true shoot-em-up style of old and combine 3D graphics on the 2d pane (just like Star Monkey), as well as being of high qualiry despit being totally free

Idinaloq is a Japanse freeware shooter that runs in the odd resolution of 512x284, but still looks good. Graphics are hardware based, but fully three-dee any very nice indeed. It also features cool anime characters, voice overs, and an opening and closing movie sequence. Worth checking out.

Vsys Gaiden is another shooter that's very similar to Star Monkey in looks and gameplay. Again freeware. Very heavy on the swarming, and the enemies are less intelligent than in Star Monkey -- instead existing in greater number at once on-screen. It's not until a few levels in that they get more aggressive (heat-seaking missiles and such.) Speaking of which, the missile effect in this game is top notch.

Can't quote any direct download links for these games, but you can find them listed at www.the-underdogs.org.
