by Geometrian on Sun May 13, 2007 7:31 pm

Try flying and killing the first squadron any way you like, then move to take out the two missile-firers. Kill them and the 6 airplanes beyond them. SHOOT AT YOUR FRIENDS and get them to run away! This keeps them from committing suicide by attacking the Red Baron.

Lead your gun over the entire next enemy squadron, then run away. Fly to the center of the map and fly in circles at a low altitude near one edge of a mountain. If anyone fires at you, go through a tunnel (I died at least 20 times this way, so practice ) and do a sharp turn at the end. If all goes well, then you'll end up with a few easy planes who weren't aggressive enough to attack you and die. These planes go down easily, though be careful not to fly through them.

The Red Baron may or may not still be alive. If he is, blast 'im. (Upside-down loops are allowed provided you return to being upside down).

All this took me hours to figure out.
