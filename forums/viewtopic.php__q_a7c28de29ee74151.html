Hi,


The Short Answer.

Not this year, but RAS 2 is coming. It's taken a lot longer than either Jon (or myself) expected, but Jon's finished building all the most difficult tools and components he needs. It's now a process of putting them to use in remaking the games. "Tower..." has some odd quirks and complex techniques going on behind the scenes, which is why it's the first to get the remake treatment, rather than one of the "Red Ace" titles. Once this game is done, the process will be well-understood and remaking the rest of our games should be a lot easier. RAS 2—and other sequels—will then follow.


The Long Answer.

RAS 2 isn't going to be a 2009 release, unfortunately. This is mainly because Windows Vista and Windows 7 have forced Jon to focus on getting most of his old games remade to work on the new versions of Windows. Nevertheless, RAS 2 is still in production—I've just completed the mission outlines for the game and will be working on the more detailed mission design too—but it's not the first in the queue for release.

(For those who aren't aware, Small Rockets used to have a number of employees, but is now a one-man operation run from Jon's home in his part time. Jon is the programmer, producer and CEO. I'm primarily the customer support guy, although I'm bringing my writing and game design experience to the table as well.)

Jon wants to get the first of the remade games out the door this year, but it'll be very tight. The title Jon chose as his first guinea pig is "Tower of the Ancients". It may not look it, but it's actually one of the more technically complex games we have and it's already proved itself a good choice as our first title: it's helped Jon spot—and fix—a number of problems with the new game components and tools he's spent the last few years building.

Jon informed me over the weekend that "Tower..." is now sufficiently advanced that he can run it on a Mac, as well as on the PC. (I'm pushing him for screenshots, but it's Thanksgiving week in the US, and he's not going to be around until after that.)

Once the new and improved "Tower..." game is out the door, the rest should follow much more quickly. This includes remakes of the existing "Red Ace" games as they're the ones giving us the most compatibility issues with Windows 7 and Windows Vista.

RAS 2's development is closely tied to that of the remakes of "Master of the Skies" and "Red Ace Squadron Pro"; there are a number of production processes which will be applicable across all three titles, so it makes sense to tackle them one after the other. RAS 2 also has the advantage that neither Jon nor myself are graphics artists. (I used to be one, but not since 1994.) That means RAS 2's new graphics can be created, levels built and refined, and the game tested while Jon works on rebuilding its predecessors.

As RAS 2 will focus on providing new levels, new mission types and enhancements to the multiplayer game, the programming itself should be straightforward once RAS Pro has been remade. It'll be a "more of the same" approach, retaining the original's core gameplay, albeit with much better graphics, new aircraft to play with, better audio, and more spit and polish generally. The gameplay itself isn't broken, so we're not going to "fix" it or risk alienating fans by making radical changes. Multiplayer games should be more fun though, as we've had a lot of very constructive feedback about it over the years.

Jon also wants to make the game "moddable", so it'll be interesting to see how that pans out.
