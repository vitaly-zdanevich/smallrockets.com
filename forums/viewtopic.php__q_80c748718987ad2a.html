In answer to YOUR question... NO. Answering... and BEING RIGHT are two different animals. Anyone can ANSWER questions.... but the real test is ARE THEY RIGHT? Here is my rule of thumb.... I try and give EVERYONE a quick answer to their post..... IF I happen to KNOW the answer, I give them that. IF it is TECHNICAL and I don't know the answer... I refer it to Sean (which he is the expert in that field). If it is a game that I have not personally played or am familiar with ... I refer it to Jonathan. A lot of ANSWERS that I post come right from the COMMUNITY itself in the POSTS that have been previously made. I have spent countless hours reading posts that date back to 2001. Based upon the dialogue back and forth, I can tell if the community suggestion SOLVED the problem or DID NOT.

Honestly, with the exception of maybe a few questions.... EVERY ANSWER has been in the FORUMS. People now a days, are just too lazy to read. It is much easier just to ask a question than to spend a few minutes seeing if the QUESTION has been ANSWERED before NUMEROUS TIMES.
