Dunno if it's too late for these or if they've been suggested before...


Offline Multiplayer

I'd like some kind of option for a random mission available. Kind of like an offline Multiplayer. Basically:
  • You pick a map
  • Choose the number and type of enemy aircraft you want to go up against (2 of those bombers, 3 of those fighters...)
  • Select a difficulty (changes how much damage they can take)
  • Hit start and have some random fun!

So rather than just re-playing the missions, you have a kind of offline multiplayer option which you can tweak to your personal tastes. It's then the same as a multiplayer game - only with bots. Each aircraft you shoot down would respawn and the first "player" to 10 wins.

So even if it's just a simple fire up a map, pick the number of opponents and go type thing, I'd enjoy it. It's just a bit frustrating at the moment when you come online to play and either no-one or only a handful of people are online.


Last Man Standing

A mode of multiplayer I'd like to see is last man standing. You choose how many lives each player has (say from 1 - 10) and rather than the first player to 10, it's the last man left alive who wins.
