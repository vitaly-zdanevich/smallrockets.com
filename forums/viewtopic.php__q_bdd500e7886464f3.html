I don't think you understood my reply. We do not HAVE the source! The game was developed by a Russian team called "D-Inter" some years ago. As I understand it, D-Inter disbanded some time ago and no longer exist.

Some rights to the games and content are with Small Rockets, but the rights to the code and engine lie partly with D-Inter -- or whomever now owns their old intellectual property -- as D-Inter used their own graphics and game code rather than our own.

In short: it's not gonna happen. Ever. Seriously. You're talking about asking people to dig through six-year-old backup tapes and deal with lawyers in *three* different countries. The sheer expense of doing that is enough to make this a non-starter; it'd cost us far more to do this than the games ever made from sales.
