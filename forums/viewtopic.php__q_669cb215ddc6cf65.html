Suggestions for New Version of RAS

by Francis Geoghegan on Sun Sep 06, 2009 9:17 pm

Rambling Lament from the dank, dismal, dungeon of dispair,

So what to do while one waits patiently for RAS2 or what ever the new version will be called.

Someone tells me about Wings of War or WoW for short. Very pretty game (Russian game folks I think) Some talented flyers on the internet. Doesnt cost much. Available as a demo from Gamespot or one of them. Newer game, but dead from support standpoint, and still wont work behind a router although works with XP and Vista 64bit and the joy stick, works even the throttle. Kind of slow and graceful sorta like flying a party balloon or like flying the feather in Forrest Gump. Yawn.

Then there's Rise of Flight, (more Russian programmers) You can actually watch a wind-stressed-surface tensioned-viscosity-tortured droplet of oil leaking from the engine make it's way past a perfectly formed rivet showing Lilly Marlane's misty reflection on the engine cowling. Many talented flyers on the internet. You can use this TrackIR thingy to see what or who is sneaking up on you. Nice feature. New game, still in a state of "flux" . It's all fluxed up too. Oops, cant play on internet behind a router and guess what? Lots of joy sticks dont work, only certain ones will work, and ya gotta have a separate throttle. Then there's that floating White Feather feeling again, and Oh you only get 500 bullets, a hand gun and a piece of string or something and you gotta watch the water temperature because it might over heat or something and the maps are big but you can compress time in case there's too much realism and you start to freak out because you are not there yet and you are tired of looking around the cockpit and the hotwater bottle you brough with you is growing cold and you are not having fun.

I hope that RAS2 or Next or whatever won't plague us with that "drifting-white-Forrest-Gump-feather-syndrome". 'N I hope that we could use a joystick including the throttle, just because it was recognized in some version of a windows control panel and maybe we could play the game behind a wireless router or maybe there could be some published work around. Um, like wait until your wife goes shopping and disconnect her computer from the internet and bypass the router and plug the wire from your computer into the cable modem and play online until she gets home.

There just ain't nuthin' out there that's like RAS. RAS has that certain cartoon-like quality with a dash of Road runner (beep-beep) and Wiley Coyote (splat!) and a certain immediate gratification factor. No one really gives a ***** about realism, we got enough of that already. Hope the new one doesn't loose its' unique flavor.

I had to get a new computer and it came with Windows Vista 64bit and a wireless modem and then there's the wireless router. I should be glad but it's a fate worse than death which is fast approaching due to I'm 70 and obami the commie wants to euthanize me.

So I'm floating around in the pretty sky over pretty scenery fetching pretty jewels so I can go pretty fast while I tow a striped gym sock while listening to some new age floating music over and over....it's like chinese water torture. (Oops is that still allowed? Maybe that's where the CIA got the idea).

I know it's hard writing programming code using a pnuematic tube to move a mechanical hand holding a pencil while confined in an iron lung but please, please hurry up. With a cherry on top even.

Regards, Wobbi
