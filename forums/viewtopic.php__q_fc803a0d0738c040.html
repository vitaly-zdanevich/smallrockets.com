HAVE THE RIGHT RAS GAME ON YOUR SYSTEM


If you do not have the Small Rockets version of Red Ace Squadron.... it WILL NOT WORK on Multi-Player in GameSpy. You MUST download the Small Rockets version to be able to PLAY Red Ace Squadron with the Multi-Player function. Their web address is:

http://www.smallrockets.com


SOLVING MOST XP FIREWALL PROBLEMS


First off....

You must have Windows XP with the SP-2 update. You MUST have the Windows XP Firewall ON.

Okay, now here goes....

Start > Settings > Control Panel > Windows Firewall > Exceptions.

click ADD PROGRAM.... find "RUN LAN RED ACE SERVER" and DOUBLE CLICK it... it will now SHOW in your exceptions list. Be SURE the BOX is CHECKED. While you are here.... if they aren't already, you might want to go ahead and add "RUN THE GAME" and "GAMESPY" to your exceptions list too BEFORE you move on. OF COURSE, these instructions are for Red Ace Squadron..... the SAME CONCEPT, however, applies to ANY GAME, INTERNET PROGRAM or SERVER that you want to ADD to the WINDOWS FIREWALL EXCEPTIONS LIST.

Any PROGRAM can be ADDED to your EXCEPTIONS LIST. Just REMEMBER that you must CHECK THE BOX for Windows to ALLOW IT.


HOW TO FIND YOUR IP ADDRESS


On your DESKTOP... you should have an ICON that says MY NETWORK PLACES. If not, do this... START > SETTINGS > CONTROL PANEL > NETWORK CONNECTIONS. Click on the desktop ICON or NETWORK CONNECTIONS, whichever way worked for you to get to this point. On the LEFT HAND SIDE you will see several OPTIONS under NETWORK TASKS. Click on VIEW NETWORK CONNECTIONS. Then, you will see One or more connection set-ups... one should say LOCAL AREA CONNECTION. Run your cursor over this and it should highlight. If you accidently CLICK on it... a window will open up, simply click CLOSE. Now... on the LEFT HAND SIDE under NETWORK TASKS at the bottom you will see DETAILS. It shows the details of what connection you have highlighted. the IP ADDRESS is all you need to provide your HOST SERVER. Also down at the bottom, you might see ASSIGNED BY DHCP. If you see this, it means that instead of a STATIC or PERMANENT IP ADDRESS, because of the amount of people on the internet at any given time (mostly cable companies), they utilize a temporary IP ADDRESS. Unless you keep your computer on and basically ON LINE all the time, if there is a gap (and it depends) of 12 to 24 hours, the ISP, when you log back on again, may assign you a NEW IP ADDRESS. They do this to free up resources from someone who logs on... logs off and stays off for a period of time.. then logs on again. Don't worry about it.... but if you go to a HOST SERVER that you previously got into before and find you can't... RECHECK your IP ADDRESS to be sure that you have not been assigned a NEW ONE


TIPS & STRATEGIES


Some of the best tips and strategies can be found at the site of one of the original masters of RAS1 and who was one of my mentors back in 2001, [RAS]BeachBum. Here is his site.

http://ra.home.comcast.net/redace/index.html

What you don't find here and in Small Rockets forums itself, you probably don't require or need. However.... since my old friend KPAX already told briefly of one of the "Secrets Of The Pros"... I thought it was time, especially with RAS2 coming out (hopefully) in a few months, to give my humble opinion about some issues, to restate some things that I have learned along the way from my mentors and to tell you about a few things I simply found out by sheer dumb luck.

NEWBIE NURTURING! Before we get started, let's briefly talk about the number one priority for the game that we love..... encouraging more and more new people to find out what a really great aerial combat game this is. Newbies will be easy to recognize.... they will be the ones shooting you on the runway. EASY... explain the ROE (Rules Of Engagement) to them. The majority I have come across (once they are told) cease and will at least TRY and follow some kind of code. If your skills have progressed and you can hold your own, don't go into a server where there are some newbies and do a CUSTER'S LAST STAND where they are getting blasted out of the skies without knowing what planet they are on. You KNOW what I mean. If you see some newbies, try and help them... either in the lobby or in the game itself. Refer them to some of the TIP AREAS where they will have a better understanding of the game and what is expected of them as RAS1 Pilots. PLAY DOWN to a level that they feel they could learn to be competitive. As an example, myself, [RAS]Xray and a lot of other pros will go to a slower plane..... FOKKER... HALBER, Etc. The thing I love about the FOKKER is that it is a ONE SHOT KILL for anyone. With newbies, I normally will just do GUNS ONLY. At LEAST they will have a fighting chance and the game won't be SO SHORT that they ask themselves "What's The Point?". Also, there is one MAJOR POINT that should be considered in NURTURING NEWBIE PILOTS..... the BIGGEST reason that pilots turn to alternative methods such as the TRAINER or some other type of CHEAT is because of SELF PRESERVATION. If they are getting MURDERED every time they get in the air they are going to LOOK for something that will LEVEL the playing field for them. THINK! NURTURE! HELP! ENCOURAGE!

1. FLIGHT PATHS/KILL ZONES. As KPAX said, most people don't realize that when you play multi-player, that the only thing that is really universal on all systems is the PLANES and the WEAPONS. The landscape, buildings, trees, etc. are only unique to your own system as far as the game is concerned. What this means is that when YOU destroy a tree or a building or a tower on YOUR MAP, it is only destroyed on YOUR COMPUTER. Everyone else still sees the existing object because they are playing using their own layout. Most of the pros will CLEAR a killing area as they fly during a game. By eliminating trees, buidings and towers (especially around pick-ups), it makes for a CLEARER flight path and also a CLEARER "Kill Zone". That is why, if you are observant of others, you will see them shoot at objects during flight. That is also why you will see bullets and rockets coming out of trees or buildings... because they have CLEARED them on their layout and you see them coming through objects that you have not destroyed. CLEAR... hehe, probably not.... but trust me, clearing your flight path will definitely put you on a more level playing field with some of the ones who already know this.

2. RADAR. It is mentioned in several areas, but not emphasized enough in my opinion. Most pros, keep one eye on the aerial combat they are in and the other eye on the radar. When I am in a multi-plane aerial combat, if I see someone coming up on my six, I will break off from the rat pack and engage the single plane coming into the group. Several of the old pros KNOW that flying close to the ground (STEALTH MODE) makes them invisible in the others RADAR. This is especially important in games where there are multiple players. You can SNEAK up on the engaged group and take out one or two before you get nailed yourself.

3. STAY LOADED. I can't say enough how important it is to NOT run yourself dry of ROCKETS or BOOST. Even if it means that you have to evade someone rather than taking them on..... even by keeping just a couple of rockets or 20% boost before you refill, that will give you a superior edge if needed. Unless you are playing my favorite type of game GUNS ONLY... if you are UNLOADED you are DEAD MEAT!

4. SMOKING=DEATH. If you see any plane that is damaged (SMOKING)... that is the WEAKEST LINK (should name a gameshow after that.. hehe). Once a plane is damaged, it can be taken out quickly with guns (depending on your plane of choice... determines HOW QUICKLY). As has been pointed out already.... in a battle.... if you have damaged the opponent it is obvious where they are headed (HEALTH PICK-UP). If you have been killed, when you RESPAWN head for the HEALTH and take them out. As most of you have already gathered.... picking up HEALTH actually can work against you in battle. [RAS]Mo will tell you in an instant that when you utilize HEALTH it really doesn't give you FULL STRENGTH even though your meter will show full.... you will take damage at an accelerated rate. Some will not utilize the HEALTH pick-ups at all and continue in battle until they are killed to REGAIN their regular health strength.

5. LEAD YOUR TARGET. As KPAX and others have mentioned, the aces in RAS have been playing the game since 2001. They know this and now you will too. ROCKETS tend to LOFT or RISE when deployed. This means that you should aim a bit LOW on your target. In addition, you should LEAD your target with GUNS and ROCKETS by a good plane's length. If you shoot AT the target, your munitions will hit DEAD AIR. BOOST and SHOOT is a must with the regulars. By BOOSTING and SHOOTING a ROCKET or BULLETS while BOOSTING... you INCREASE the SPEED of the ROCKETS and/or BULLETS. This can be extremely important in a close match. It just takes a little practice.

6. SECRET MISSION. If you have beaten the game in MISSIONS MODE without using the stupid trainer or some modified cheat... CONGRATULATONS.... if NOT.... shame on you! Get rid of the cheats and get back in that cockpit, for the MISSIONS MODE is the groundwork to being a COMPETITIVE RAS Pilot... for RAS1 and RAS2. Once you have successfully DONE THAT ..... WITHOUT CHEATING..... you will have access to the SECRET MISSION and this is your perfect training ground for more ADVANCED COMBAT! Although I think there is a tip somewhere of how to make your game where you can access the SECRET MISSION directly, TRUST ME, you will GAIN NOTHING by going the BYPASS ROUTE going to the SECRET MISSION and then going directly into MULTI-PLAYER. But if you get here HONORABLY, this is where you can practice LEADING your targets with GUNS and ROCKETS. It will also give you great EXPERIENCE with multiple plane scenerios. Any pro will tell you that playing the MISSIONS MODE COMPLETELY and entering the SECRET MISSION is the perfect TRAINING GROUND for RAS1 and RAS2 when you want to LEARN at your OWN PACE before engaging REAL LIVE OPPONENTS. And quite frankly, if you have beaten the game with HONOR, you are already well on your way to being a REMEMBERED and HONORED ...... RAS PILOT! (Salute)

7. IMMELMAN LOOP. Any pilot worth his salt will tell you that one of the most critical things a pilot must do in order to be successful in aerial combat is the Immelman Loop. Check out this site. It SHOWS you how to do this.

http://www.furball.warbirdsiii.com/krod/ACM-immelman.html

8. AIRSPEED. Being able to ADJUST your AIRSPEED during combat is critical. By lowering your airspeed in turns or loops, you can actually SHARPEN your turn and overtake your opponent. Depending on your aircraft, you will need to play with this a bit and find out what you can and can't do successfully with different aircraft.

9. AIRBRAKES. Yes... RAS1 aircraft DO have airbrakes. This is the technique and how to do it when you are being pursued by another aircraft. Reduce your AIRSPEED to ZERO. If they are close on your tail, they will fly in front of you and you can take them out. Also, if you are boosting and someone is boosting after you, reduce your airspeed while boosting to zero... when you stop boosting they will shoot past you because you will come to a slow stop.

10. DEAD KILLS. Once you have been killed, you are still capable of taking someone out (DEAD KILL). Depending on the plane, you can stay in the air from 10 seconds (Junker) to over 5 minutes (Airco). It works better with a joystick than a mouse. The technique is simple, when you are killed, pull back on your stick and head straight up. If you have some boost left... great.... When you get to the ceiling (and as you are looking at your radar at the targets available), you simply lower your nose and head for the action. Obviously, having ROCKETS is critical. Most of the pros can take out at least one or two depending on the amount of aircraft still in aerial combat. My personal best... in an Airco... is 4. Most of the regulars also know... that if you kill an experienced pilot... go HIGH. Once they start down, they can't go back up and thus you avoid being a DEAD KILL.

11. ROCKET TECHNIQUES. Most of the pros can hit a target of their choosing from great distances.... because they know from experience, how long it will take their rocket(s) to hit a designated impact point from a certain distance (normal or boosted). They AIM for WHERE they think the target will be when the rocket(s) arrive. They also employ what is called a VOLLEY or a SPREAD. Like a submarine commander, most pros will shoot a spread of several rockets at a designated impact point, which greatly increases the probability of a hit or kill. With a little practice, by yourself, you can practice different shots from different distances and can quickly improve your game and kill ratio. Of course, these techniques are used for long distance shooting.... close in aerial combat is just a matter of leading your target.

12. VIEW KEYS. Without question, the MASTER of this is my old friend [RAS]Xray, who was also one of my mentors back in 2001. The VIEW KEYS are the ARROW KEYS. If you have one of the prorammable joysticks, you can program your TOP HAT to do these functions. The advantage of the VIEW KEYS can be impressive. Although it may take some getting use to, it will give you a better idea of what the person you are in aerial combat with is doing and intends to do. [RAS]Xray is the number one seed in RAS1. So, he is doing something right.

13. DON'T BE PREDICTABLE. For the seasoned pro, it won't take them long to learn a pilot's tendencies. Like any HABIT, people get comfortable doing things the same way. This can be DANGEROUS against a pro. Once they identify what your TENDENCIES are in aerial combat, it gives them a tremendous advantage. My suggestion to you is to CHANGE UP from time to time what you do in combat.

14. DON'T PANIC! I'll never forget my first time when I found myself in a one on one with my mentor [RAS]Xray. I was so nervous, my heart was pounding and I was so hyped up, I killed myself by flying into the ground, trees, buildings and pick-up points about as much as [RAS]Xray killed me himself. As my experience increased, these feelings gradually vanished and I became COMPETITIVE. The POINT is that you are ALWAYS going to come across and compete against SOMEONE who will beat you the MAJORITY of the time. So.... DON'T PANIC...... remember that by playing the BEST PLAYERS your OWN GAME PLAY will improve DRAMATICALLY along the way. LEARN from the pros. WATCH what they DO. Have a question? ASK THEM. The benefit of this game is NOT to be an ACE.... but to be COMPETITIVE, HAVE FUN and MAKE FRIENDS!

15. KEEP THE GAME GOING! With the exception of games where there are several ACES or PROS in it and the difference in a victory can be a single shot or a few seconds difference between dual kills of each other (YES... there CAN be 10 to 10 TIES if both pilots are killed at the exact SAME TIME)...... if you are in a game where you have a 4 or 5 kill lead.... when you get to 9, rather than go after your 10th kill and restarting the game over..... exit out and then come back in. The majority of the ACES and PROS have long since passed the NEED for a victory. They fly because they love the competition, the game and the people. EVERYONE KNOWS that you earned the VICTORY..... but it EXTENDS the gameplay and is more fun for everyone. Nothing is more BORING than playing a game for 2 minutes before someone gets to 10.


SECRETS & FUN


1. UNDERGROUND. In case you are one of the FEW who don't already know, COUNTRYSIDE has a flaw in the graphics program. Using the MAIN ROAD through the mountains as your guide... there is a ROCKET PICK-UP on one side and a BOOST PICK-UP on the other side. On the BOOST SIDE where the OCEAN meets the WATER CANAL you will see a opening where the land meets the water. If you fly at a reduced speed and hit the seam in the middle with a little down angle, you will find one of the neatest things in RAS1. You will be flying UNDERGROUND. You can see all the objects ABOVE GROUND and you can even FIRE your ROCKETS or GUNS at objects on the surface from underneath. Many of us have had battles UNDERGROUND. You will find things to be a bit different underground... you have to be so close to the surface for your weapons to work.... you can actually DIVE into the depths of HEAVEN (all white)..... you can fly UP through the ground and reappear on the surface.... (watch out for obstacles)... you can get ROCKETS and BOOST underground by flying close to them underground.... just about everything.... lots of fun if you want something different to do.....

2. LANDINGS. Most of us have LANDED our planes on the mountain peaks in countryside and desert. You can also land your planes in the tunnels in DESERT. This is lots of fun but it takes some timing and skill to master these. But, with time on your hands... this will be a good challenge for you. I find the AIRCO to be the all around best for this because of the control you have with this plane.... however... different planes for different people... experiment and see what you can achieve for fun.

3. INVERTED COMBAT. If you want something to do that is different, try a game of INVERTED COMBAT (upside down). It really strengthens your flying skills. If you can master making pick-ups and some kills INVERTED, then you are ready for most in RAS1.

One last thing.... COURTESY and HONOR. The majority of pilots in RAS1, I am proud to say, fly with a certain ROE (rules of engagement) and COE (code of ethics). Here are the basics......

1. NO RUNWAY SHOOTING! Let people get off the ground. There is ample time to kill them IN THE AIR.

2. NO PICK-UP SITTING! Don't inch your plane under a pick-up station. If you MOVE your plane to this position, you are considered a CHEAT and will probably get CLOBBERED by the others. Most consider this action as FAIR GAME since you have moved your plane from the designated RESPAWN area and are not taking off.

3. COURTESY! If you see that a plane has taken off and is heading for a pick-up, DON'T ENGAGE them. If they ENGAGE YOU, then all bets are off and you can kill them at will. And this is AIMED at a few of you..... the DUMBEST THING that you can do is ENGAGE a plane that you know is LOADED. If they allow you to pass by.... don't be stupid, take the courtesy and go fill up before engaging others. The majority of the pros will allow courtesy pick-ups when games begin, etc.

4. ENGAGE or NOT! The majority of the regulars will NOT engage someone who is CIRCLING a pick-up point. Normally, if someone is CIRCLING, it means they are unloaded and trying to get a load. If you are circling for a pick-up DO NOT FIRE YOUR GUNS.... the majority will not fire on you unless YOU ENGAGE FIRST. Now... for the most important part of this. IF someone passes you by... and moves on.... DON'T shoot them in the REAR if you get a load as they are flying off. This defeats the courtesy that you were given (and will piss off a bunch). Trust me when I tell you that THEY will REMEMBER the NEXT TIME if you VIOLATE their COURTESY!

I hope you can learn something from some of these. Remember, the main objective of the RAS community is to have FUN, to play with HONOR and to MAKE FRIENDS! Just don't use the damn trainer or other cheat!


CREATING A PRIVATE SERVER (NO CHEATERS)


First off....

You must have Windows XP with the SP-2 update. You MUST have the Windows XP Firewall ON. You MUST have the "RUN LAN RED ACE SERVER" added to your program exceptions list. If it is NOT on your exceptions list... click ADD PROGRAM.... find "RUN LAN RED ACE SERVER" and DOUBLE CLICK it... it will now SHOW in your exceptions list. Be SURE the BOX is CHECKED. While you are here.... if they aren't already, you might want to go ahead and add "RUN THE GAME" and "GAMESPY" to your exceptions list too BEFORE you move on.

Okay, now here goes....

Start > Settings > Control Panel > Windows Firewall.

Click EXCEPTIONS

Highlight RUN LAN RED ACE SERVER. This should be already on the program exceptions list and CHECKED.

Click EDIT

Click CHANGE SCOPE (it probably is checked for ANY COMPUTER)

Click CUSTOM LIST

Now all you have to do is put in your approved IP ADDRESSES. You MUST separate each IP ADDRESS by a comma (NO SPACES). Once someone has logged onto your computer, you will note that their SUBNET MASK has now been added behind their IP ADDRESS. Don't freak out, this is normal. Once you have finished adding the IP ADDRESSES, then click OK and you are done. You can ADD or REMOVE someone's IP ADDRESS even if your server is up and running. Up or down... makes no difference.

If you have added your IP'S incorrectly, you will get an error message with a reminder about the comma, etc. Basically, NO SPACES and separate by a COMMA. When you go back in to add more people, simply CLICK on the IP AREA and then use your RIGHT ARROW KEY to speed to the right (END). Then, put your COMMA (no space) and log your next IP.

Almost forgot the BEST PART. This can be done for ANY GAME that has a SERVER PROGRAM. American's Army, Ghost Recon, Wings Of War, whatever. Miss the adventure of the cheaters and idiots from hell... NO PROBLEM! Go back to CHANGE THE SCOPE and click ANY COMPUTER again... and there you are. All the IP'S that you did on CUSTOM will still be there when you regain your SANITY!

A SPECIAL HINT just for those SPECIAL ONES.... create SPECIAL SERVERS for SPECIAL THINGS. Want to always play GUNS ONLY and know that only a FEW share your love of authentic adventure..... COPY the LAN SERVER...... change the NAME to whatever... GUNS ONLY, BALLS ONLY or something just as manly, add it in the programs exception's list.... and when you WANT..... start IT. Of course, this is assuming that you have already ADDED the IP'S of the ones who share this love.

Like I said, hope this gives some relief and ray of hope until RAS2 or a game that is CHEAT PROOF comes along. At least with this little tool, you CAN take back over control of WHO and WHAT plays in your own BACKYARD!


If you have a problem... let me know and I will try and help you.

One other thing.... because some ISPS utilize DHCP to assign IP ADDRESSES, if someone's computer goes down for more than a 12 to 24 hour period, they may get assigned a NEW IP and would have to let the HOST know of their NEW IP ADDRESS.

Have Fun, Watch Your Six.... (Salute)
