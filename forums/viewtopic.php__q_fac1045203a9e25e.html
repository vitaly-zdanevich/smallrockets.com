Hi folks,

This is just a reminder to all of you who have bought a game from us to remember your account passwords!

Our accounts are defined by a combination of email address and password. If you forget your password, PLEASE click the "Forgotten your password? Click Here." link on the login page! The server will automatically send you an email to remind you of the password.

If you decide instead to create a new account with a different password, your license code(s) will not appear in this account! We will need to 'merge' it with your old account to make your license codes appear again. This requires human intervention, so it can take time to sort out.

Also, don't forget that it's possible to update your email address and even change your password if you wish. The links for these options appear on your account page when you login.

This has been a Public Service Announcement on behalf of Small Rockets.
