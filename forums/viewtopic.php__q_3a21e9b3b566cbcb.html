Hi,

Small Rockets are going to be updating their internally-developed titles to be XP-compatible. (Some already are.) It'll take a few months to complete this process, after which they'll crack on with some new games.

Externally-developed titles cannot be updated in this way; SR have no access to the program code and the development teams might even have split up. These games include both the "Jetboat" titles and the "Hot Chix" series as well as "Extreme Kayaking". These were all developed by a bunch of coders and artists based in eastern Europe who are no longer in contact with SR. Sadly, these are also the titles that have given the most problems with XP, although users running Windows 95, 98 and ME can still play them.
