1. Learn how to 'juke' (ie: swing back and forth while still heading in the same general direction). If you juke for awhile, your adversary won't be expecting you to continue the turn and you'll be able to get behind them.
2. Listen. If you hear machine-gun fire, or rockets exploding, it means someone is sneaking up on you below radar. Take approprite action.
3. Pay attention to how people react when you fire at them head-on. Some people will always break left or right. By sending a missile about a planes-length to that side, you can use their technique against them.
4. Put your opponents on the defensive as soon as possible. Someone trying to avoid being shot-down does not represent much of a threat.
5. Go after weaker prey. If, on your radar, you notice someone break off from a fight and head towards a power-up, cut them off at the pass.
6. Learn the respawn time for power-ups. If you can keep your opponents from collecting them, you will find your battles becoming MUCH easier.
7. If someone is juking in front of you, leading you towards a power-up, aim for the power-up. They ALWAYS try to slide in and grab it at the last possible second.
8. Don't advertise your plans. Whenever possible I ALWAYS try to fly below radar. CAUTION: this can be used against you. One of my favorite tactics is to follow someone flying below radar. He doesn't even know your there and makes for a very easy (and satisfying) kill.
9. When approaching a fur-ball (ie: multiple targets engaged in combat) pick your targets carefully. I try to attack in this order:
-targets with missiles should always be dealt with first.
-the most skilled player should be taken out next, ignore all others until this is done.
-targets flying towards you, kill 'em before they can get behind you and cause problems.
-targets flying away from you represent the least threat and should be dealt with last.
10. Blast 'em on the runway. It's dirty and frowned upon, but hey, it works! :)
