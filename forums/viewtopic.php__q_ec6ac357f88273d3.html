******* New Central5 Challenge

by Snoopy on Thu May 10, 2007 9:17 pm

With a Junkers, I was able to complete the Central5 Master/Ace mission without using guns. Zero kills and zero rounds fired! (took-off at the start and never landed, and no ceiling flying)

Get enemy planes to chase you. Circle buildings, oil wells, and mountains. If you do it right, they will crash. Save your bombs for the tanks and buildings marked red on your radar. You won't have enough for AA guns, and you can't bomb a plane because that will count as a kill. If an enemy won't engage in battle, try bumping its wing. Hopefully he won't die when you do because that will count as a kill.

Remember, you have to end up with zero kills and zero rounds fired in the Master/Ace version of Central Mission 5.
