Ever wanted constant boost??
Pick your plane...
Go to the plane.ini file for the selected plane;
Make a copy;
go to [physics];
adjust climbspeed, cruisespeed, divespeed, highspeed ect. as nesasary (try doubling, but dont be greedy you wont be able to control the plane);
this will double your speed, obviously..

Have a play with diferent settings, if you discover anything interisting please post it here

Note: Sometimes adjusting features can have unexpected adverse effects. eg. When you increase firerate your fireing range is reduced, firerate=1000 will bring it down to about 2 plane lenths, this is fairly useless.

Tip.. Make a shortcut to the planes folder for easy access, and try manny diferent configurations to find the best one
