bry wrote:
by the way what other drivers are critical to make the game work i dont want to download stuff i dont need


The drivers you need are the primary drivers supplied by:

1. Your graphics card chipset's manufacturer (e.g. ATI, NVidia, SiS, Intel, etc.)

2. Your sound card chipset's manufacturer (e.g. Creative Labs, Realtek, Cirrus Logic, Terratec, Intel, etc.)

3. Your motherboard's manufacturer (e.g. SiS, ASUS/ASUSTEK, VIA, ATI, NVidia, Intel, etc.)

4. Your input device(s)' manufacturer(s) (e.g. Logitech, Microsoft, Trust, etc.)


Note that a "Codec" isn't a driver. Our games don't require any weird or unusual codecs. All PCs should have any codecs our games use installed by default. It's the drivers which are key, although for most people, no driver updates should be necessary.

If your PC has a very new graphics or sound card, such as an ATI X1800-series graphics card or Creative Labs' X-Fi sound card, you may find that the current drivers are unstable as it can take a while for driver issues to be ironed out.

There is, sadly, nothing much we can do about drivers as these are out of our control. This is the price we pay for the PC's open architecture.
