There IS something you can do by a bit of programming to eliminate that damn "windows key" which sits between your "ctrl" key and your "alt" key on the left side of your windows keyboard. I KNOW... because until I eliminated it.... it RUINED more games than I could count when FLYING in aerial combat, and accidently hitting it instead of the "ctrl" key to reduce my throttle.

The simplest and fastest solution.... POP OFF THE DAMN KEY! I have it sitting in a drawer if I ever need it again (not in this lifetime). Since the "windows" keyboards have that square seat inverted hole, even if you hit the area where the key was.... there is nothing there to activate it. However... if you REALLY are SMART and are REALLY BRAVE.... then HERE is how you do it, programming wise.

How To Disable Windows Key (Contributed By [RAS]BeachBum)

This tweak disables the Windows key that is found between the Ctrl and Alt keys on a Windows enhanced keyboard. Ever been playing a game and mistakenly touch the Windows Key? Everything goes to heck in a hand basket, and you consider yourself lucky to get out of the situation without re-booting. Even then, all is lost, game over, you lose. Do you really use the Windows Key anyway? Honestly, I do (or did), but only because it happened to be there. It really didn't make things convenient enough to warrant leaving it active and putting up with the problems and annoyances it causes. Here is how to 86 it.

a. Open the registry.

b. Navigate to Registry Settings System Key:
[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Keyboard Layout]

c. Create a new Binary value, or modify the existing value, called "Scancode Map" and set it to:

00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 03 00 00 00 00 00 5B E0 00 00 5C E0 00 00 00 00d. Exit the registry. You may need to reboot or log out and back in for the change to take effect.

NOTE: You can not copy/paste this data into the registry; you will have to type it in. Be careful and double check your work. Also worth noting is that I don't normally pass on tweaks that involve messing with the Registry due to the risks involved. The exception I am making here is a testament to how important it has become for gamers to know how to get rid of this game crasher.

Registry Settings

System Key: [HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Keyboard Layout]

Value Name: Scancode Map

Data Type: REG_BINARY (Binary Value)

Value Data: 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 03 00 00 00 00 00 5B E0 00 00 5C E0 00 00 00 00

(Default) REG_SZ (value not set)
Scancode Map REG_BINARY 00 00 00 00 00 00 00...

HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Keyboard Layout

Disclaimer: Modifying the registry can cause serious problems that may require you to reinstall your operating system. I cannot guarantee that problems resulting from modifications to the registry can be solved. Use the information provided here at your own risk.
