I think it would be amazing if the campaign had a simple multiplayer mode for two people. Add a few more enemies, divide resources between players, and go!

This could be LAN only or internet.

When one player died, the other could finish the mission on their own, or they would have the option to restart with the dead player. Players would be resurected at the end of each level.

Also, about the Campaign, it was good in RAS, but needed more variety. Maybe one mission could be destroy the plane, another be destroy a building, another escape through a series of valleys (flying too high would result in being spotted by anti-air guns), one mission could be solely reconosissance, and a very few could be destroying all planes.

A map creator for multiplayer would kick tailfin.

Perhaps too solve the problem of cheaters, you could make a seperate "realm" where cheats would be allowed, and a regular server.

Also, a ranking system could help eliminate cheating.
Players create an account, start off with a score of 5. When you leave a game, other players would have the ability to mark you as a cheater, which would decrease your score, or as a legit, which would increase your score.

Servers could be configured to block people with too low of a score.

The game sound really awesome, I CAN'T WAIT!!!!!!
