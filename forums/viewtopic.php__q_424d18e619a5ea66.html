Well we were putting alot of effort into Ether and Deluge, quite a while back now. Deluge became the GBA Wakeboarding Unleashed game. Project Ether was prototyped, but we didn't get it picked up by a publisher in the end.

Whilst this was going on we started a new project with Atari - Sinbad: Legend of the Seven Seas for the PC, and most of the last year has been spent on this project which has recently been released. Because of the size of the project and the short timescale this took up all of our efforts.

Moving forward we are going to be focusing on ESD game developments - certainly initially on the PC. There is no ESD market per se on the current generation of consoles. Publishers are only interested in AAA big buget titles for consoles - which are increadibly hard to land, and not really what Small Rockets is all about.

So the long and short of it is that we don't have any console titles in the pipeline currently, I'm afraid.

What I can say is that I played the Crimson Skies game at E3, and thought it was pretty good Its not really like a squadron, as it is a bit more simmy in feel - certainly terms of how the planes feel to fly. It is very accessable tho.
