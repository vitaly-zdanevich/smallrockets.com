I think Jon is of the same opinion: Zeppelins were (crap) bombers. They were also huge, slow gasbags full of extremely flammable hydrogen. They were quite capable of exploding all by themselves while <i>landing</i>, let alone while being shot at, so their value in a war situation is pretty much nil.

The only way for Zeppelins to make sense in RAS 2 would be if they bore very little resemblance to their real-world counterparts.

RAS 2 will, fundamentally, be more of the same. Which is what most of our customers seem to want. The graphics engine will be much more advanced and the missions should have a bit more variety, but ultimately, it's all about the dogfighting. WW1 fighter pilots weren't doing the same things as those in WW2. Most WW1 flying was for recon purposes rather than bombing raids; in that, at least, the "Red Ace" games have already bent the truth a fair bit.

by [RAS]xray on Sat May 26, 2007 4:20 am

Hi there....I also have Vista(home premium) and Gamespy runs just fine with it
To ensure you can get in to Gamespy with Vista...please do the following.
Right click on the Gamespy icon once you have downloaded and installed it.Select properties.Go to the Compatibility tab.Put a check in the box for"run this program in compatability mode for"....select Windows XP service pack 2 from the pull down menu.Then go to the bottom of that dialog box and put a check in the box for"run this program as an administrator".
You are all set and should have no problems logging in to Gamespy (and playing RAS Multiplayer after that.)
Once you have logged in you'll need to get into the RAS chat room via a player spy link as the final staging room link to our chat room won't work with Vista(yet).
To do that I suggest you wait in the main chat room (the one you go to when u log in) and ask someone from [HELP] to put you on their player spy list....then have them go into the RAS chat room and you'll be able to click on their link and get into the room.
Once you are in there you will see an "options" menu which will allow you to select"make this your startup location".
After that,every time you log in you will automatically be taken into the RAS chat room without further ado.
ps...beating everyone on LAN may not quite prepare you for taking me on in RAS multiplayer
Best of luck...hope to see you in the skies soon,
