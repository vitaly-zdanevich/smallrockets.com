Hi,
Go online to our web site at http://www.smallrockets.com, click on "Account" and you will be taken, via a login page, to your personal account details which will include the license codes for any games you have purchased from us.
Note: You must login using the name and e-mail address you used when purchasing the games.

-OR-

Send a mail to feedback@smallrockets.com containing:

The name of the product purchased
The date and method of purchase
Your name exactly as used when purchasing the game
Your e-mail address, exactly as used when purchasing the game

The staff should then be able to locate you in our database and provide you with your license code again.

Regards,
