Re: When is Red Ace Squadron 2 being released

by stimarco on Tue Jul 07, 2009 1:49 pm

As soon as it's ready.

Neither Jon (the boss), nor myself have any more idea of a specific date than you do. Jon's tried setting deadlines, but something always came up to derail his plans, so he's decided not to tempt fate yet again. Jon completed work on the new game engine RAS 2 earlier this year, but he is now using it to remake some of our current games as this is the only viable way to make them fully compatible with Windows Vista and Windows 7. This process should help iron out any quirks or bugs in the new engine, without the added distractions of having to design new levels or specify new graphics.

RAS 2 itself has been in development for some time now, so there's already a fair bit of new artwork ready for it. Work has merely been temporarily suspended while the remakes are made. Jon aspires to have the first remake essentially completed within a few months, but this isn't a guarantee. He's chosen "Tower of The Ancients" as the first remake as it has a number of unusual elements which should push the new engine and reveal any gaps in its features. Jon is also posting regular updates to his "developer diary" thread in these forums. (Jon doesn't normally keep a diary, and he also has a day-job, so please don't expect regular daily or weekly updates!)

Jon is no more interested in having this drag on and on than you or anybody else.
--
Sean Baggaley
SR Support.
