RAS2 FAQ (Updated January 27, 2006)

by stimarco on Sun Sep 19, 2004 2:12 pm

Any Frequently Asked Questions will be added to this thread over time.

Without further ado, here are some questions & answers:

1: "When will RAS2 be released?"

A: Summer 2006.

We've started essentially from scratch with this title, so we're going to have a good 'tool kit' we can then use for future titles, RAS2 and other RAS sequels.

We expect to start testing in the first quarter of 2006 by implementing RAS2 Modules into RAS PRO for testing and feedback. Final release of RAS2 being Summer 2006.

2: "What will be in RAS2?"

Good question. So far, we intend to include the following as a bare minimum:

* Improved network play support over both LAN and Internet
* Improved control configuration support
* A built-in level / mission editor, so you can make your own levels
* ALL of the options that are in RAS PRO right now
* Lots of SECRET STUFF which we don't want to disclose

3: "Can I be a tester? Huh? Please? Can I?"

We have already selected a small group, most who have been playing the game since it's release in 2001. Because of their length of time and familiarity with the game, they will be doing our pre-release testing. However, if you have upgraded for free to PRO if you bought the original RAS here at Small Rockets or you have purchased PRO as your FIRST GAME, you will be able to TEST some of the RAS2 features as PRO will be the testing vehicle for RAS2 modules. Once the TEAM has tested, it will be made available FREE to PRO OWNERS for their testing on our Home Page here, and you will be able to give us your input on the consumer test forum.

4: "Can we post suggestions to this forum?"

Absolutely!

5. "Will you read them?"

Certainly! We can't guarantee a reply to every post, or even every individual thread, but we do read suggestions made here. Your programmer for this game is Jonathan (HQRAS) and he has already replied to a few posts here.

6. "Will there be any hacks and cheat modes?"

NO! One of the biggest GRIPES that were part of RAS before RAS PRO was designed.... were CHEATERS! RAS PRO, and it's ANTI-CHEAT technology has made a world of difference in the game and the multi-player community inter-action. That said, there will be included, like was done in PRO.... LEVELS OF DIFFICULTY.... where one can play the game and get all the way through it on the "rookie level" with little difficulty. Just like the new PRO, RAS2 will have 4 levels of play.

7. "Any chance of screenshots and progress reports?"

Why yes! Check out the RAS2 Minisite linked to from the Small Rockets home page.

http://www.smallrockets.com/pc/redacesquadron2/

We'll also post updates to relevant threads in the RAS2 forum.
