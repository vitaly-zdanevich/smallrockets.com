Brad....

PRO is an enhanced version of the original Red Ace Squadron game. PRO has several levels of difficulty (which the original game did not have) as well as a tremendous amount of EXTRAS if you wish to experience multi-player through the GameSpy community.

Your ORIGINAL validation of your purchase would automatically give you PRO for FREE. If you cannot remember or find your code, you can, through the home page, go to ACCOUNT and follow the instructions there. There are also some shortcuts to click on in the event you can't remember some of the things it asks. If all that fails..... you can click on SUPPORT and send SEAN everything that you can remember about your e-mail and personal data.... and he can see if he can find it.

PRO is the "starting" game now in the franchise. The original game is no longer available as PRO "is" the original with enhancements.

There will be one more UPGRADE which is PRO2 that will be FREE if you have a registered, purchased PRO (whether it be from an upgrade or a first purchase). Once PRO2 is fully tested... the NEXT game to come out will be the unveiling of RAS2. which will be for SALE as a stand alone game between $19.95 and $29.95.

Hope that helps.
