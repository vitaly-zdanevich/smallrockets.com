Re: Still Waiting for RAS2 + Hi to all

by stimarco on Sat Feb 27, 2010 9:53 am

Hi,

One problem is that any new maps would need to be created by someone who can design a good level and balance it properly . This isn't as easy as it seems, and Jon is already up to his eyeballs in work—both for SR and for his day-job.

The other reason is that the "Red Ace" games are built on a game engine that dates back 10 years now. They're simply not running reliably enough on Windows Vista and Windows 7 for us to be comfortable putting them back in the shop window again. We get enough complaints from people asking why the game they just bought won't run on their swanky new PC, and triggering refunds is a time-consuming manual process Jon really could do without!

If Jon keeps getting side-tracked, RAS 2 will never be completed. I guarantee it. There are only so many hours in the day, and Jon would need to find—and pay—someone to create new levels for RAS Pro, test them, wrap it all up in a nice, friendly installer, and update the website too. Remember, it really is just Jon doing this. There's nobody else. I don't even live on the same continent! (In fact, I'm currently typing this on a Macbook Pro in a hotel room in France!)
--
Sean Baggaley
SR Support.
