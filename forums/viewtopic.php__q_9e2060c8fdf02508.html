Re: RAS 2

by stimarco on Thu Dec 11, 2008 4:40 pm

So! It has come to this -- literary allusions! How the mighty have fallen!


Folks, I freely admit -- having known Jon personally since our school days in the early '80s -- that Jon is a programmer first and a writer / communicator somewhere around... uhm... 4219th. Believe me, he is working on a new game, but Small Rockets is a hobby projects these days, not a full-time day-job. Making games is his first love, so RAS 2 will happen. I just don't know when. And neither does Jon.

The current plan is to pick a simple game -- probably "Tower of the Ancients" -- and rebuild it using the new engine as a test case, to see how well the engine holds up. If all goes well, the most popular games in the SR catalogue will be updated to use the new engine -- essentially rewriting the old games, but recycling some of the original assets -- so that we can cut down on support problems and finally, officially, support Vista and the forthcoming Windows 7. (And other platforms!)

RAS 2 (and more) will follow once those are out of the way. (Plan A had been to get RAS 2 done first, but this isn't tenable now; we're seeing a lot more requests for Vista support.)

If it's any consolation, I'm working on a few game projects myself. (No, it's not RAS 2, though "RAS 2D" might be a good fit. )

Re: RAS 2

by stimarco on Fri Dec 12, 2008 8:56 am

A quick update: I just had an online chat with Jon -- we live on separate continents now, so I haven't seen him in person since he moved to the US in 2005 -- and he gave me a quick sitrep. He also asked me to apologise for neglecting the forums for so long. As he put it himself: "It's hard to wax enthusiastically in a public forum like this about issues like collision-detection and scene graph design patterns." (That Jon likes to write these things for fun is, I think, all that needs to be said on the matter!)

That said, the current situation is that the engine is now 99% done and he plans to port "Towers..." to it as a test next month. This will flag the end of the long, long haul and the beginning of the final sprint to the finish for RAS 2. We do apologise to all of you for the long hibernation, but spring is just around the corner.

Meanwhile, down in the SR Forums...

Conzul wrote:It would be fun to have a MMO Ras Game, even 2D


It's not beyond the realms of technology, so I don't think Jon would rule it out. (I'm not sure how well a 2D MMO in this vein would do. I'm still prototyping "RAS 2D" -- which I can now officially confirm as the working title -- so I can't even be certain at this stage if my design will work at all yet. There's still some experimentation and testing to go.

An MMO dogfighting game based around the current (3D) "Red Ace" games would be challenging to design. I'd probably be inclined to go for an explicit competition theme, with leagues and knockout rounds, etc. There are existing competitions, such as the old barnstorming events and the more recent Red Bull Air Race -- http://www.redbullairrace.com/ -- which can act as templates.

Hmm...

(wanders off to have a good think.)
