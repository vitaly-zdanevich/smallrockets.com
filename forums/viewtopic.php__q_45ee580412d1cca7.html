Bullets Only and Bullets, Bombers & Rockets

by Francis Geoghegan on Mon Jul 23, 2007 3:18 pm

Yep it is and they are.

You may create your own server on gamespy and play in any format that suits you. I have created Bullets Only and Bullets, Bombers and Rockets for my own particular enjoyment.

Start your RAS Server program and then disconnect. Select preferences and enable or disable the features that you want and then save and exit out.

Go to Gamespy and click on create game and continue on with prompts and viola!, you have your own room. There will be lag sometimes, but nothings perfect, so you'll just have to play through it.

I am on US East Coast play in the evenings. You are welcome, as is everyone, to join in Bullets Only and/or the Bullets, Bombers & Rockets.

Winning or loosing isn't my bag, it's the flying, dying and trying that is the fun part, so there's no rules (It's a free for all) except: You probably dont want to shoot anyone on the ground, although, some do, and no whining, LOL.

Regards, Wobbie
