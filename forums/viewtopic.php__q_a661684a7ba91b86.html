The D-Inter-developed games were only tested on XP through to SP1 and we saw occasional hardware compatibility issues even with that. SP2 seems to have killed them on some machines, while SP3 has fixed a few of those, but broken others. As we have no access to the source code, there's little we can do to fix problems short of fiddling with the installer itself. (That said, I've seen these games running more stably in Vista under its new "Compatibility Mode" features, so that might be worth looking into.)

There's a very good reason why we list those games as "unsupported": we really have no way to change the games. They were designed and built by a Russian team for the Windows 95/98/ME platforms, almost eight years ago. That team has made it clear they have absolutely no interest in updating their games for current platforms, so our hands are tied. We're just the publisher for their titles; we didn't develop them and we have no source materials we can play with to try and fix the problems.
