LOL… well, I must say I have learned to let it all roll right off. WTF taken literally means "What The Frekeldefrak" right? I play with hundreds of players everyday; my multi-player hardened mind doesn’t interpret a lot of acronyms literally any more. I keep one very important thing in the forefront of my thinking when playing online; diversity.

Man, are we diverse… so many differences, global, age, all sorts of backgrounds and should I say rearing, and the list goes on. I just thank God, or whoever we are suppose to thank these days for just still being around and having all the online friends to play these games with.

So, my answer would have to be “none of the above.” I do get a little annoyed when someone spams the chat with mindless chatter in an obvious attempt to get someone to finally ask them to shut up, giving them the excuse they were looking for to go berserk.

This brings voice comms to mind though. When there is also a voice comm involved, most of the bad guys get taken care of in due course. As you know, I prefer actually speaking with people. We all actually get to know each other a little better, the community becomes self governing over time… and it is a lot more fun to speak with your friends, get to know their family, and so forth versus just typing on a computer screen.
