How To Host RAS On Internet

Posted: Thu Apr 01, 2004 12:09 pm
by blucas1379
After playing RAS for many months I have just now discovered how to host a game on the internet without going through GameSpy. First click on this link to find your IP address then write it down so you do not forget it, be sure to put the periods in the proper places.http://www.chami.com/tips/internet/041498I.html

You then got to Start then Programs then Small Rockets then on the RAS tab click Run Modem Red Ace Server. You leave that on your desktop along with the other black box. You then click Run The Game then when that opens up click Multiplayer. Then click Change IP Server and type in your IP address then click enter. You then click Internet Connect and you are on your way, have fun.
