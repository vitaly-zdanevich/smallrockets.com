Re: joystick

by stimarco on Mon Oct 15, 2007 11:24 am

tim the ace wrote:How do you use a joystick with this game?


Plug it in and adjust its settings in the appropriate Control Panel thingy.

This is assuming it's a standard USB joystick or joypad. Neither of the "Red Ace" titles are designed as hardcore simulations, so please don't expect every single button on your 19-stick, 233-button überjoystick to work!

If the joystick is _almost_ working, take a look through the FAQ forum as there is an article in there on tweaking the Joystick.INI file. This technique seems to fix most sensitivity and/or axis selection issues.
