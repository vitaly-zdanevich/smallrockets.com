Red Ace Squadron overview

The highly acclaimed 'Red ace Squadron' that have turned GOLD! Are such a game that everybody would enjoy!, but some set backs occurred in the game it self that damages the potential of the 'Small Rocket' game developers, the supposedly better game than the famous 'Master of the skies: The red Ace' turned out to be one poor game that could use some more development time before it final commercial released!, the rush to publish it or launched it as soon as possible maybe the point that will bring the game to untimely short life duration, the strong spots that were present in the M.O.T.S failed to be carried to the R.A.S GOLD, The menu it self needed to be more simple and more customizable ,the heavy eye candy in the main menu and the pre-loading stages created a single player lag for a low end machines which were not present in the M.O.T.S which carried a simple but sufficient main menu and pre-loading stages!, the lack in the customizing of controls could plague novice gamers, even thou a simple 'ini' file editing could do the job for an expert gamer, the quality of backgrounds of buildings and other structures was very excellent compared to the big budget MSN 'Fighter ace' game, which carried a heavy program around 50 to 70MB in download,that will deterred any modem gamers from downloading the game!,but the M.O.T.S and the R.A.S game carried only around 20MB of files with anything under 30MB are very reasonable for modem gamers, the R.A.S GOLD missions was very good for a serious simulation gamers, but using the same map/scenario for a second expert level is not going to help the game at all!, bringing to the public that it was a premature released of the game, or somewhat lack of ideas by the developers!.
The innovations of creating the game from a simple design how ever should be admired by all,making the file smaller than it's competitor in the simulation world!, the explosion of planes being shot in the air have been better in the R.A.S but the ground impact of a falling plane that should be a 'brownish' explosion with black smokes created by a plane hitting and scattering the ground dust were replaced by an un real MSN fighter ace/ Hollywood style of explosion! , and no choice of plane or only 2 choice of planes in the inventory is the worst thing in the game!, thus creating a tempting challenge for a serious gamer of simulation to create modding to allow them to choose more planes(which we have done and tested with great potentials , but constrained to a single PC for evaluation purposes only!, thus respecting the full authority and copyright of Small Rockets as a developer, the game would be restored to it's original configuration after the final evaluations are done),the game are a registered game to our member.

We highly encourage the Small Rocket team to create and launch a FREE add-on map pack of at least 12 maps/scenarios for the R.A.S GOLD version, to restore the expectation of game buyers on quality of Small Rocket games!, we also encourage the Small rockets team to release a 'one click' map generator, for R.A.S and M.O.T.S as a shareware that cost around $5.00 for a 30 day trial, as a value added bonus for buyers.

Proof, if such were needed, that you cannot please all of the people all of the time.

FYI:

The features you've asked for are certainly feasible, although I'd debate the value of some of them, but they will all cost money.

Small Rockets is a very small development team which just happens to have its own online distribution system. (Sales from retail channels are unlikely to make much money for SR. Their games are priced as budget titles. Retailers and distributors between them will suck up to 80% of the money. Remove the taxes, currency exchange fees and banking charges. and you're left with little more than loose change.)

Like it or not, you can't expect a small development team like SR to produce titles with the same budgets as Microsoft Flight Simulator. They just don't have that much money.
