by Geometrian on Thu Jul 05, 2007 2:11 pm

For those who don't know, the ranks listed below your name are administered by the forum code and (in a few cases) by forum admins. The ranks are based on the number of posted messages as follows:

0 - 9 posts = Aircraftman
10 - 29 posts = Senior Aircraftman
30 - 49 posts = Corporal
50 - 74 posts = Sergeant
75 - 99 posts = Flight Sergeant
100 - ? posts = Warrant Officer

Updates to come.
