New Development Diaries Section!

by jonathan on Tue Jan 13, 2009 8:36 pm

Sean has been bugging me recently about not doing any updates. He's right of course. So he suggested I do a development diary.

I was a bit reluctant at first because every hour doing a diary is time taken away from doing actual development. But thinking about it a bit more I think its a great idea, and I know its been embarrassing long between updates. And yes I know its been embarrassingly long since we've released anything.

Well I'm going to do it, and try a be more open about whats going on during development. To do this I've added a
Development Diaries forum under the General Discussion area. I hope to update it on fairly regular intervals (certainly better than once a month).

The first diary entry can be found here!

Thanks for sticking with SmallRockets. I know its slow and painful process, but there is light at the end of the tunnel.

Hope you find the development diary useful, and hopefully it shows we are getting there. Really

Thanks,

Jonathan
