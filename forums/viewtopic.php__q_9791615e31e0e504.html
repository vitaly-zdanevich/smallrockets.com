I have the Windows version of SR Poker, and I am also disappointed that I could find any servers for it. The game's AI is to weak, and I would really like some on-line human players to compete against.

The problem with the AI is that it TOO EASY TO BLUFF! That is odd because in all the other computer poker games I've played, bluffing the computer is usually difficult.

In SR Poker all you have to keep doing is bet that maximum of $100 every time you bet. 95% of the time tthe 3 AI players will all fold. The few times they stay in is when they have a good hand. This "always bet the maximum" trick works best on 7 Card Stud, Texas Hold'em, and Omaha because the large number of betting rounds makes it easier to force out any AI player that wants to stay in. Because 5 Card Draw has only 2 rounds of betting, this trick is hard to use on that game.

I have wiped out the funds of all 3 AI players using this trick. When they all run out of money, a window pops up that says "YOU HAVE WON, THE OTHER PLAYES CANNOT CONTINUE!" Yes, that's how it's spelled, "PLAYES."
