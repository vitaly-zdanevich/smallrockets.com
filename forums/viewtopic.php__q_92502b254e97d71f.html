The Jetboat series is now free. (Check the "free stuff" button on the SR homepage.)

Note that you will need to follow the instructions on unlocking the demos carefully as the codes are set against your name (as displayed in your Small Rockets account). I'm not sure if this means the older codes are no longer accepted by the demos, but it may be the case.

Please be aware that the Jetboat games are _not_ supported under Windows XP. There are known issues with these games under this version of Windows and there is nothing we can do about it -- that's why they're now free.
