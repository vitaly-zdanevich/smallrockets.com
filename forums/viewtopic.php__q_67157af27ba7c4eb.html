Unfortunately, Small Rockets have very little say in how RealArcade market their wares. Their license to sell our games was granted many years ago, back when nobody really knew what all the repercussions would be. Which is why Jon no longer licenses his games to other parties.

Sadly, what's done is done. And legally binding at that, so there's little we can do except advise people to get a refund from RealArcade if their version doesn't meet expectations.

RealArcade are aware of RAS Pro's existence, but they appear unwilling to sell it. I suspect this is because our games appeal to a different audience than they are now used to. (Most of their games are of the ilk of Bejewelled, Zuma, etc. They're good fun, but aimed at the abstract / puzzle game market rather than our preferred simulationist sector.)

I wish there were more I could do, but my hands are tied.

Handing out license codes is a big no-no, but we're not complete mercenaries. That said, I would rather the 14-year-old affected by this issue contacted me through our support system rather than trying to blag a license code from someone else.

Giving your code away will cause problems down the line as Jon intends to replace our licensing system when RAS 2 is ready.

Regards,

--
Sean Baggaley
SR Support.
