Directions

By Train - there are four fast trains an hour from London Waterloo to Guildford (08, 23, 38, 53 minutes past the hour - destination Portsmouth Harbour). Journey time is around 35 minutes.

By Foot - the Small Rockets office is a ten-minute walk from the station. From the main exit of the station, walk down the hill to the one way system, and turn left over the bridge. Cross over at the traffic lights, go right along the edge of the Friary Shopping Centre, and proceed up the hill (North St). Small Rockets is situated above Laura Ashley on the right hand side near the top of the hill.
