Console development

Small Rockets is in the process of developing cutting edge games content for next generation console platforms including the Sony Playstation 2, Nintendo Gamecube and Microsoft XBox systems.

Currently we have 2 next generation console prototypes in development, together with a Game Boy Advance title. Small Rockets has a philosophy of producing titles which are accessable to a wide audience, and most importantly, fun.

We have extensive experience of developing for a wide selection of different game genres.
We are keenly aware of the importance of how a game plays, as well as how it looks and sounds.
Equally, we appreciate the importance of the factors that make a game commercially successful.

The next generation console titles use our new proprietary game technologies to deliver exceptional gaming experiences. For the moment we are unable to release too many details on the developments, because of contractual obligations, and because we don't want to give away too many of the surprises quite yet.
