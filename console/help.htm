Why don't you have more information about your console developments?

On some titles we are working on advanced prototypes and seeking publishers. Its therefore too early to show details on the games, or give away too much about what the game designs we have in the works.

On titles singed to signed to publishers we need to work closely with the publisher in order to be able to release information.

As soon as we can release more information on the projects we will do so and make as much of it as possible available directly from our website.

What about consoles and ESD?

For the moment the majority of consoles in consumers homes do not have online connections. This is changing with Sony introducing its broadband service and Xbox ships as standard with a broadband adaptor. Therefore at the moment the industry is only at the very early stages of ESD on consoles.

First there must be the technology infrastructure to make such a service available, and then a service must be built. This service needs to provide a catalogue of high quality games that can be downloaded and purchased very simply. As yet such a service has not been made available to the public. Small Rockets is working hard building relationships and technology for these services.

When will 4x4 offroaders be released?

The offroaders project began as a technology demonstration - showing that you could do real-time undulating 3d landscapes on the GBA. Some of that technology has now been moved over to our new game "Project: Whirlybird" - although this game is not an off-road racing game. For the moment we are concentrating on "whirlybird" and so 4x4 is taking a back seat.
