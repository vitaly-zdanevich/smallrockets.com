Founded in October 2000, the Small Rockets team consists of around 20 people based in Guildford, UK. With some of the most talented and diverse game developers on the globe, Small Rockets has over 15 years' experience in the game industry.

Games

The Small Rockets team has wide experience of working in the PC and console markets bringing several titles to retail for PC and Dreamcast. Our artists and programmers have worked on a wide variety of titles, including:

  • Syndicate Wars
  • Deep Fighter
  • RedLine Racer
  • Suzuki Alstare Extreme Racing
  • Gunman Chronicles
  • Trickstyle
  • Magic Carpet 2
  • Theme Hospital.
With a broad range of knowledge of technologies and platform specific expertise, Small Rockets has a comprehensive wealth of resource to tackle next generation platforms and handhelds.

Technology

Small Rockets also has a prestigious pedigree within the technical world through its Managing Director, Jonathan Small. Jonathan has worked in 3D apps and libraries for over 10 years, including his work in creating Small Rockets' proprietary, platform independent game engine. The engine's abilities are far reaching, including compression, encryption, e-commerce, networking, sound, interactive music, 2D and 3D rendering.

In the past Jonathan worked for Criterion Software. There he created the 3D game libraries used for Sub Culture and Redline Racer. These libraries have become the basis for Criterion's middleware product 'Renderware 3'.
