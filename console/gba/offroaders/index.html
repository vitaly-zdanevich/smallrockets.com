Off Road Racing

The first fully 3D racing game for the Game Boy Advance!

4x4 Off-Roaders is a frantic 4x4 racing experience. Tracks are fully modelled in 3D, with hills, valleys, lakes and ravines - stay on the track or explore off-road, it's your choice.
With 3 tracks, 12 cars and 4 player link-up play, 4x4 Off-Roaders takes hand-held racing games to the next level!

Check out the video and the screenshots and see it for yourself!

If you want to download the movie, click here.

What is it?

  • An arcade-style off-road racing game with realistic physics and graphics

Features

  • Seat-of-your-pants racing on offroad dirtracks and hilly environments
  • Three different and massive environments - desert, snow and country
  • Race 3 AI competitors at once
  • Three classes of offroad vehicle - trucks, 4x4s and buggies - all modelled on real vehicles
  • 12 individual vehicles, each with unique look and handling characteristics
  • Link up option for up to four players to compete together

Game Modes

  • Player league - complete challenges within certain targets to open up later tracks, challenges and vehicles
  • Variety of challenges - race, capture the flag, time trial, pickup challenge
  • 4 player link up

Technology

  • Real-time textured 3D terrain technology - on the Gameboy Advance!
  • Free form landscape - you can drive anywhere, not just on the course
  • Realistic 3D physics, combined with fun handling model
  • Fully 3D modeled vehicles - can rotate and be seen from all angles
