Console

Small Rockets is working on two next-generation console developments for Xbox, Gamecube and Playstation 2. Since both projects are in the prototype phase we cannot reveal too much information publicly about the titles. Rest assured, when we can release more information you will find it here.

As a developer we have a wide range of development experience, including development on a variety of console platforms. In order to allow our developments to having cutting edge gameplay, graphics and sound, we are working hard on a diverse set of technologies. Both of our new developments use this new technology as their main building blocks.
