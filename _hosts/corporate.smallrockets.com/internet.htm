In addition to the development and publishing of games, Small Rockets has a range of other options for content portals such as Internet Service Providers.

Resellers

Becoming a Small Rockets reseller is an easy and lucrative way to add gaming content to your portal.

Games are offered for download and purchase from the reseller's site, with the reseller collecting revenue directly via their own e-commerce delivery system. Codes are automatically generated from the Small Rockets website, and we take a percentage of the revenue on each game sold, but otherwise it's up to you - the more games you sell, the more revenue you get!

To find out more about becoming a reseller, contact us at corporate@smallrockets.com.

Payment systems

Our e-commerce system has been developed to be fully flexible and "payment system-agnostic". This means that it can be adapted to work with a wide range of different payment systems and methods to suit our partners' requirements. For example, our system is currently compatible with the JALDA payment system and Splash Plastic, a credit card alternative for young people.

Web games

In addition to reselling downloadable games, many portals now offer a range of simple, classic games which can be played direct from the web browser. We have already developed a full range of web games, and welcome enquiries about these use of these games or bespoke development of new titles. (See also Brand-led content.)
