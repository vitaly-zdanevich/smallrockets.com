 - Partners &  alliances

 







Small Rockets actively seeks to develop its business and promote its products through partnerships and alliances with publishers, developers and other like-minded organisations.

Some of the people we're currently working with are:

Real Networks set up its downloadable games site (www.real.com/games) in March 2000. The site is part of the Real.com Network, with access to over 160 million registered users of the Realplayer media player, and now has over 40 original PC titles available for download.

There are currently 5 Small Rockets games available from the Real.com site, including The Red Ace, Tower Of The Ancients and Jetboat Superchamps, which consistently feature in the top ten downloads from the site. Small Rockets has also recently signed a deal with Real Networks to sell new releases such as Art Is Dead and MAD on the site.

Global Star Software (www.globalstarsoftware.com) is a subsidiary of Take 2 Interactive which publishes and distributes boxed retail titles in North America and elsewhere through retail channels such as Babbages, Electronics Boutique and Wal-Mart. Global Star currently publishes 3 Small Rockets titles in boxed format for the North American and Canadian markets, and a further 3 titles are forthcoming by the end of Q1 2001.
Gameplay (www.gameplay.com) is the UK's leading destination for gaming content and online sales of games software. Small Rockets has signed a deal to make its downloadable games available via Gameplay's "Instant Gameplay" electronic distribution area; the first title, The Red Ace, should be available by March 2001.
Xtend Software (www.xtendsoftware.de), part of the ThyssenKrupp Information Services group, is a publisher of retail and electronically-distributed entertainment software in Germany. Xtend currently has agreements with Small Rockets to distribute five titles online, and the publisher also sells these games in boxed form.
Jalda (www.jalda.com) is a method of transacting online payment that avoids the difficulties and security issues of using credit cards. The system can be used by any company which has a billing relationship with their customers. Small Rockets has integrated the purchasing system into its current catalogue of 10 titles designed specifically to meet the challenge of ESD.
Splash Plastic (www.splashplastic.com) is a new prepay card aimed at UK teenagers who want to shop online without a credit card. All titles available from the Small Rockets site are available for purchase via Splash Plastic.
