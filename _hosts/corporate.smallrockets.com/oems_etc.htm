 - OEMs / CD distributors


 





When buying hardware consumers increasingly expect software too. Many new PCs and peripherals now come with bundled applications or entertainment software, which can dramatically enhance the attractiveness of the overall offer.

Providing attractive software can be a challenge, particularly when adequate documentation and appropriateness for the target audience are taken into account, but Small Rockets titles are ideal for inclusion on bundled CDs. Their compressed format means that they take up little space yet are accompanied by full documentation. Most importantly, their content is targeted directly at a broad mass market audience, and thus a strong appeal to a cross-section of hardware buyers.

Distributors of CDs can also benefit from including Small Rockets titles on their releases, particularly on magazine cover mounts. Small Rockets titles have appeared on the coverdiscs of a number of major publications, including PC Zone, PC Gamer and PC Format.

For more details on including our titles on CDs, please contact corporate@smallrockets.com.
