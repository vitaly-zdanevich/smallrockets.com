Small Rockets is committed to ESD, or Electronic Software Distribution. Customers obtain and purchase all our games online, with the minimum of hassle and delay.

For our range of PC titles, customers can download demo versions of the games, try them out and, if they like what they see, purchase them instantly online. Demo sizes are kept to around 15Mb to avoid long download times. Purchasing occurs in a seamless fashion using a license code system via our website's e-commerce capability. Following links in the games, customers go online, enter their credit card details and are supplied with a unique license code, which when entered into the demo immediately unlocks the full version of the game - no further downloading is required. Thus free trialing and subsequent purchase are integrated into a seamless whole.

Our ESD titles do not require broadband. Due to the small file sizes and compression techniques used, users can make full use of the advantages of ESD even on narrowband connections.

The advantages of ESD are clear.

For publishers and developers, there are no distribution or packaging costs, cutting out the middle man and creating better margins through zero incremental costs. As these costs do not have to be passed onto the consumer, games can be sold much more cheaply while still creating significant revenue.

For the customer, it provides quick access to exciting and innovative software titles, with little or no waiting around, at attractive prices. And the introduction of broadband networks will only improve the ease and simplicity of access to electronically distributed content.

In addition to the PC demo model, we're exploring new delivery and purchase methods which utilize the latest advances in the technology. For example, all our games are now compatible with the JALDA payment system, which bypasses the need for credit card transactions altogether by making use of existing billing relationships with the customer, and Splash Plastic, a payment card for young people who don't have access to a credit card.

As broadband becomes ubiquitous, and ESD establishes itself as the norm for software distribution, Small Rockets will thus be ideally positioned to lead the way with our proven approach and technology.
