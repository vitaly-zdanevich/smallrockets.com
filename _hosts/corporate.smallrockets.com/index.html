Small Rockets was set up in October 2000 to create innovative entertainment software for electronic distribution. Founded by Jonathan Small, previously head of Fiendish Games, the company comprises a team of 16 programmers and designers based in Guildford, England, and is dedicated to exploring the opportunities for interactive entertainment created by the digital communications revolution.

The company specialises in producing high-quality games titles which are distributed and purchased entirely online via the Small Rockets website and other content partners such as Real.com Games. The aim is to produce smaller and more accessible games with the quality and production values of traditional retail titles, whilst utilising the cost-savings offered by online distribution to provide excellent value for money.

Small Rockets also develops AAA titles designed for traditional retail markets on next-generation console platforms (PS2, XBOX, GameCube, Game Boy Advance).

Small Rockets is working with a variety of platforms that utilise digital communications technology, including set-top boxes, web browsers and mobile phones. The company is also developing proprietary technologies to facilitate the distribution and creation of online interactive entertainment.
