Six boats, three tracks and an ocean of water!

There's nothing like speeding at 300 mph across a treacherous water surface in a jet-powered speedboat. That's why there's nothing like Jetboat Superchamps. The rules are simple - be first, soonest, fastest!

Take control of one of six crazy Manga-inspired jetboaters, from the noble-but-stern Takeshi to the flirtatious Kaori, and race around the Aqua Marine, Urban Mist and Shrine courses. Beat the competitive Champs in the Championship mode, or challenge yourself against the clock in Time Trial mode.

The only prizes for runners-up are a mouthful of water-spray and an empty wake!

Overview

Your boat is both powered and steered by a jet of water. It has the most maneuverability when you are accelerating. Take your finger off the accelerator and you may just drift into the shore. You must try and navigate through buoys placed around the course. If you miss three in a row you will be thrown out of the race!

The game takes place in three different environments

  • Aqua marine - in the crystal clear waters
  • Urban - through the canals of an industral complex
  • New world - through tribal villages, and epic buildings
Your craft also has a turbo ability. This resource is limited - the more gates you successfully navigate the more turbo will be available.
