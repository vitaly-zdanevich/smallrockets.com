Introduction

Last holiday season, Harry thought he'd finally killed off any Xmas cheer by blowing Old Saint Nick to kingdom come. But this year, Harry's discovered that some of Santa's minions are still around, bringing happiness to children everywhere, and our hunter's vowed to get them all!

So Harry's got a plan - he's disguised himself as his old enemy, so this year Santa's gone postal!

How to play

 If Harry's going to put the spirit of Santa to rest for good, you're going to have to guide him through ten new levels in the ice and snow.

Shoot everything that moves and collect corpses to fill your vat - fill it before the time runs out and you've beaten the level!

Remember to keep an eye out for carelessly discarded pieces of military hardware - it may be Xmas, but you're going to need all the arsenal you can find...

Don`t forget that your prey in NFK lead their own lives (well as near to real as polygons and textures can get) so be prepared to see them eating each other as well as getting it on to create more hapless victims for your meat obsession!

Have fun, and don't rest until the Elves are all done for!

Controls

or  
Use the cursor (arrow) keys to move - you can also use W,A,S,D

On a French installation of Windows the keys W,A,S,D are replaced with Z,Q,S and D

Use the mouse to look around

or
Right shift key (or space) held down while moving makes you run

Left mouse button, left shift key or insert on the key pad fires your gun

Right mouse button aims (use forward and backward keys to zoom in and out)


Keys 1-4 change weapons


Keys 7-9 make animal noises (to attract prey)
