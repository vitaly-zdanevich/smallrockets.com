Poker can take on many forms and it has become a highly evolved game of great skill. Unlike other card games, Poker relies not only on the player's ability to place cunning bets, but also, the player must be able to strategically out-maneuver their opponent in a strange dance of raises, calls, and checks. It is no wonder that terms such as 'poker face' have leaked into our mainstream when we talk about people lying or bluffing!
