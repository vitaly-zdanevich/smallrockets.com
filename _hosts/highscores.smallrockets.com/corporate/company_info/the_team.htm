Company Info

ESD development

Small Rockets is the world leader in developing content designed specifically for Electonic Software Distribution (ESD). To date the company has produced over 17 titles for the PC. All of these titles are designed to take into account the technical as well as content issues required by this new and dynamic market.

Retail game development

SmallRockets developed Sinbad: Legend of the Seven Seas (PC) for Atari/Dreamworks. The game is a fighting/combat game with a novel mouse control system and viewpoint. The game features many assets directly from the feature film on which it is based. The game is designed to take advantage of the latest Hyper Threading Technology (HTT) from Intel, to improve the graphics and physics effects. Sinbad: Legend of the Seven Seas will be released in the US in Q4 2003, with a release in Europe taking place shortly afterwards.

Small Rockets developed Wakeboarding Unleashed featuring Shawn Murray (GBA) for Activision. The game follows on in style from Activisions hugely popular Tony Hawks Pro Skateboarding. Being towed behind a speeding boat provides its own challenges and gameplay features. The game uses an advanced voxel based rendering system to allow the GBA to produce smooth textured 3d graphics. Wakeboarding Unleashed featuring Shawn Murray will be released through Aspyr in Q4 2003.

Technology

Small Rockets has a prestigious pedigree within the technical world through its Managing Director, Jonathan Small. Jonathan has worked in 3D apps and libraries for over 10 years, including his work in creating Small Rockets' proprietary, platform independent game engine. The engine's abilities are far reaching, including compression, encryption, e-commerce, networking, sound, interactive music, 2D and 3D rendering.

In the past Jonathan worked for Criterion Software. There he created the 3D game libraries used for the games Sub Culture and Redline Racer. These libraries have become the basis for Criterion Software's middleware product 'Renderware 3'.
