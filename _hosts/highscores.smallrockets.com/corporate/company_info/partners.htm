Partners

Listed below are some of the partners we are currently working with on ESD and retail products.

Activision

- www.activision.com

Small Rockets developed the GBA version of Wakeboarding Unleashed featuring Shaun Murray. Wakeboarding Unleashed will be published by Activision as part of the O2 extreme sports range in the near future.

RealNetworks Inc.

- www.real.com/games

Real Networks set up its downloadable games site in March 2000. The site is part of the Real.com Network, with access to over 160 million registered users of the Realplayer media player, and now has over 40 original PC titles available for download.

There are currently 7 Small Rockets games available from the Real.com site, including Red Ace Squadron, The Red Ace, Star Monkey, Tower Of The Ancients and Jetboat Superchamps, which consistently feature in the top ten downloads from the site. Small Rockets has also recently signed a deal with Real Networks to sell new releases such as Art Is Dead and MAD on the site.

Globalstar Software

- www.globalstarsoftware.com

Globalstar Software is a subsidiary of Take 2 Interactive which publishes and distributes boxed retail titles in North America and elsewhere through retail channels such as Babbages, Electronics Boutique and Wal-Mart. Global Star currently publishes most Small Rockets ESD titles in boxed format for the North American and Canadian markets.

Tornado Group

- www.tornadogroup.com

Tornado group provides e-commerce and digital distribution services. Their technology solutions are used currently by www.bol.com and they have a selection of high profile partners in the works.

Softwrap

- www.softwrap.com

Softwrap has proprietry software wrapping technology. A selection of Small Rockets games are available through this technology at

  • FreeServe
  • 5 star shareware

Freeloader

- www.freeloader.com

Freeloader will be making a selection of Small Rockets games available for purchase in the near future.

Hi2

- www.hi2.com

Hi2 provides a game aggregation service known as Playology. A selection of Small Rockets games will be available through this service through Tiscali.

Stream Theory

- www.streamtheory.com

Stream Theory provides an online PC games streaming service. Through Stream Theory a selection of Small Rockets titles can be found in a streamable form on the following portals...

  • Adrenaline Vault
  • GameZone Online
  • GameDemo.com
  • GameGenie
  • Gamer’s Pulse
