Game Technology

Small Rockets has a firm foundation in software engineering and technology development. Some of the engines and technologies the SmallRockets team has created include...
  • 'Dive Engine' - used in RedLine Racer and Sub Culture and the basis of 'Renderware 3.0'
  • 'Renderware Playstation' - the port of the original RenderWare to Playstation
  • 'Dive 5' - Fiendish games ESD/games engine - used in all of the Fiendish developed ESD games
  • 'Dive 6' - Small Rockets 2nd generation ESD/games engine - used in all of the Small Rockets developed ESD games
