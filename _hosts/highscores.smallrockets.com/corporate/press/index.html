Press information

For up-to-date press information on the latest releases from Small Rockets, make sure you're on the press mailing list. You can join this by emailing marketing@smallrockets.com - please send your name, the name of your publication/organisation, email address and fax number.

Registered members can also gain to access to full press resources for each game, including features, screenshots and artwork.

If you have already received access to our private press area, you may enter here.
