The Small Rockets team consists of 30 employees world-wide with offices in Guildford, UK and St. Petersburg, Russia. With some of the most talented and diverse game developers on the globe, Small Rockets has over 15 years of experience in the game industry.

Small Rockets has gained its years of expertise through 3D technology, full price retail PC and console games, web development, and the budget PC market:

Technology

Small Rockets has a prestigious pedigree within the technical world through its Managing Director, Jonathan Small. Jonathan has worked in 3D apps and libraries for over 10 years, including his work in creating Small Rockets' proprietary, platform independent game engine. The engine's abilities are far reaching, including compression, encryption, e-commerce, networking, sound, interactive music, 2D and 3D rendering.

In the past Jonathan worked for Criterion Software. There he created the 3D game libraries used for Sub Culture and Redline Racer. These libraries have become the basis for Criterion's middleware product 'Renderware 3'.

Full Price Retail Games

The Small Rockets team also has experience of working in the PC and console markets bringing several titles to retail for PC and Dreamcast. Our artists and programmers have worked on such titles as Trickstyle (DC, PC), Deep Fighter (DC, PC), Suzuki Alstare Extreme Racing (DC, PC), and the much acclaimed RedLine Racer (PC), to name a few. With a broad range of knowledge of technologies and platform specific expertise, Small Rockets has a comprehensive wealth of resource to tackle next generation platforms and the PC market.

Web Development

Small Rockets includes within its portfolio several high profile members of the web community who have transferred to the games industry. Pete Lane, head of web development, worked previously for the BBC as project manager on the award winning BBC news website, http://news.bbc.co.uk. In addition, Shawnee Sequeira, director of development, started her web career at Agency.com where she produced sites for Take 2 Interactive and the RSPCA.

Budget Market

Eleven original downloadable PC titles are currently available from Small Rockets, with five more to follow in 2001. Small Rockets is one of the pioneers of ESD (electronic software distribution), and titles are available from the Small Rockets site, Real.com Games, Gameplay.com and other major retail sites. Successful games published by Small Rockets in this format include Master Of The Skies: The Red Ace, Tower Of The Ancients and MAD: Global Thermonuclear War.
