The Small Rockets aim is: to exploit the new interactive entertainment opportunities enabled by the convergence of digitally connected computing devices.

Currently, the games market is a deluge of over-bloated products that very much cater to a hard core gaming audience. These types of games involve large amounts of people and monetary investment and often have a very minute chance of becoming a big hit game. Typically, less than 10% of these types of games see any profit whatsoever.

In many respects, these large "epic" games are comparable to big blockbuster movies. Again, much staff and money is required to produce this type of content with the possibility of making any money relatively slim or even non-existent.

In contrast, Small Rockets games are more in line with the type of production that goes into TV programs. We intend to produce a new kind of content similar in philosophy to that of TV content. TV content is generally more self-contained and has simpler production values and therefore, has less risk in regards to return on investment. This, however, does not mean that TV content has less value than film content - it is merely different content with a different agenda.

A typical Small Rockets title will be

  • designed to be distributed over a digital network
  • cost an order of magnitude less to produce than an "epic" game
  • accessible by a mass market audience
  • creative & original
  • fun
Just like TV, we believe this type of game will be one of the main interactive entertainment content types used by the masses. Digitally distributed games are available directly into the home with content trimmed down to accommodate a more mainstream consumer. In this way, this kind of "wired" content does not compete with the likes of epic games and both game types can co-exist just as TV and film do today.

The aim of Small Rockets is to be at the forefront of this revolution, and through our products and technologies to be regarded as the premier destination and source for a new kind of digital content which, in our view, will quickly dominate the market for interactive entertainment.
