Game development is an uncertain quantity for publishers as well as developers. The production of epic games requires large advances with little guarantee that the returns will justify the investment of development and marketing costs, given the extended time frames mandatory for such projects. And when games finally reach the shelves, distributors frequently take a healthy cut of the final revenue from the customer.

As proven by our successful relationships with Real Networks, Global Star and other publishers and distributors, Small Rockets presents a dynamic alternative to the traditional development model. Radically reduced development times, mass-market appeal, reduced risk and an open approach to licensing all combine to produce a unique opportunities for publishers in the following areas:

Online

With 8 titles already available, Small Rockets is one of the pioneers of ESD (electronic software distribution) for Internet connected PCs. We have created a proprietary system for the delivery of gaming content for trial by customers, with instant purchasing and payment online.

ESD presents a number of clear advantages over traditional distribution. The Internet is a market of over [X] billion users, unhindered by national borders. Product can shipped direct to consumers, bypassing the costs of real-world distribution networks and other middlemen. Internet consumers participate in communities which can generate their own momentum for products through word of mouth and positive feedback. Our games are designed to take advantage of ESD's strengths. The trial demo system allows potential customers to sample the product free of charge, with purchasing made easy and attractive once the demo game play has them hooked.

All games are constructed as small downloads to circumvent current problems with narrow bandwidth, so even on home modems customers can get quick access to demos. Finally, the content of the games is carefully developed to appeal to a broad mass market audience rather than a hard-core minority - ideal for the wide demographic currently engaged in Internet use.

For the publisher, ESD cuts down on overheads such distribution and packaging costs. More importantly, ESD products have a greatly extended shelf-life in relation to boxed retail product, since they do not have to rely on remaining physically prominent in store - every new visitor to an ESD site potentially sees all games promoted equally, regardless of the length of time the game has been available.

Small Rockets would be interested to talk to potential publishers about both development deals for new original titles, and publishing agreements for games from our extensive back catalogue. If you'd like to talk to us, contact Jonathan Small at corporate@smallrockets.com.

CD versions

Although the games are designed for electronic distribution, full versions can easily packaged and sold in the standard boxed CD-ROM format. Successful CD versions have already been produced in the US, Canada and Germany, with more territories to follow.

CD versions include a specialised CD interface, better quality streamed music and bonus game demos of other Small Rockets titles. Due to their size and content, these versions make excellent budget titles for mass-market consumption.

As boxed retail rights are frequently assigned separately from ESD rights, we welcome enquiries from publishers interested in either or both forms of distribution, for either development or re-publishing. Contact corporate@smallrockets.com.

Localisation

Due to their emphasis on immediate playability and simple game models with little need for explanatory text, Small Rockets titles make excellent candidates for localisation into languages other than English. Several titles have already been successfully translated into German, and localisation is currently underway for the Chinese market.

Small Rockets does not have a localisation team in-house, but we have full localisation kits prepared for each game and would be happy to advise on the most effective approach to converting the games.
