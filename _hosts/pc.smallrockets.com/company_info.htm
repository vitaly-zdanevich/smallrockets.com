Small Rockets was set up in October 2000 to create innovative entertainment software for electronic distribution. Founded by Jonathan Small, previously head of Fiendish Games, the company is dedicated to exploring the opportunities for interactive entertainment created by the digital communications revolution.

The company specialises in producing high-quality games titles for the PC which are distributed and purchased entirely online. Using the shareware model, games are downloadable in demo form from the Small Rockets site and other locations, and can be purchased using a license key arrangement via Small Rockets’ e-commerce system. The aim is to produce smaller and more accessible games with the quality and production values of traditional retail titles, whilst utilising the cost-savings offered by online distribution to provide excellent value for money.

Through a licensing agreement with Criterion Software, eight downloadable PC titles are already available from the Small Rockets website, including successful titles such as Master Of The Skies: The Red Ace, Jetboat Superchamps and Tower Of The Ancients. More titles are currently in development, with at least another four titles (Jetboat Superchamps 2, Art Is Dead, MAD and Kayak Extreme) scheduled for release before Q2 2001.

In addition to selling software via its own site, Small Rockets has relationships with a number of major publishers. Real.com Games, the downloadable games site from Real Networks, currently features four Small Rockets titles, and the company’s next four releases will all be premiered exclusively via this service. Boxed versions of several games from the Small Rockets range are published in the US and Canada by Global Star Software, a subsidiary of Take 2 Interactive, with more releases planned. Finally, there are additional agreements with Gameplay for electronic distribution of current titles, and Fun ‘n’ Game for distribution in China and Hong Kong.

As well as PC titles, Small Rockets creates games for a variety of other platforms such as set-top boxes, web browsers and mobile phones.

Small Rockets is based in Guildford, England, and comprises 14 people. There is also an external team based in St. Petersburg, Russia.
