Contact us

Before contacting us please check for answers through the Frequently Asked Questions below. It is also worth checking the support pages and our forums, both of which have a wealth of information about common problems and questions.

IMPORTANT!

To contact us you must have an account on the website. Don't worry its quick, easy and free to set up! If you don't have an account, please create one. If you already have an account please login. Once in the account pages go to the 'messages' section.

If you are trying to contact us about one of the 'free games', we do not provide any support for these games. If you send a support message about such games we are unable to send a reply. For support you should try the support pages for the game, the general support pages, or the forums. You can find more information about the free games here.

Not receiving replies from SmallRockets?

We generally aim to reply to any message within 3 working days. Occassionally we get a large influx of mail and it can take longer. If you do not receive a reply within a week, then there may be another problem.

If you've tried to contact us in the past but didn't receive a reply, your ISP or e-mail program may be blocking e-mails from SmallRockets. Try consulting your e-mail program's help pages to find out how to change or remove the settings that filter or block e-mails. It may also be worth contacting your Internet Service Provider or System Administrator to ask them why you're not receiving smallrockets.com e-mails.

Note that you can always access your SmallRockets mail messages directly on the SmallRockets website by going to the account pages. If there is no reply there - then no reply has been sent.
