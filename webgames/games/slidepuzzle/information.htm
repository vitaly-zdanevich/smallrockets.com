Sliding Puzzle

This is a recreation of the classic '15' game, the objective being to restore each of the 15 sliding tiles to their original places.

Click on a tile to move it into the neighbouring space - the game is won when the picture is restored.

About Sliding Puzzle

This game utilizes JavaScript to work within web and set-top box browsers.

It is easily modifiable to use alternative tile sets and has a minimal overhead ensuring a slick experience on any platform.
