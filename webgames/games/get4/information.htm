Get 4

Line 'em up to win - get a line of four in any direction (vertical, horizontal or diagonal) before the computer does to win!

About Get 4

This game utilizes CGI scripts to work within web and set-top box browsers.

The game features sophisticated Artificial Intelligence with varying difficulty levels, and can also operate as a two player game.
