Battleships

Defeat the computer by sinking all his battleships!

At the start of the game, you must place your ships. To do so, click on a square on the main board where you want one end of your ship to be.
Arrows will indicate possible places where the other end can go, so click on one of these to place it.

Once all five ships are placed, the computer will place it's ships.

During play, click on a square on the main board to launch an attack. If you have hit one of the computers ships, you'll see an explosion. Otherwise, you'll see a splash as your ammunition hits the sea.

If you sink all the computer's ships before he sinks yours, victory is achieved.

About Battleships

This game utilizes JavaScript to work within web and set-top box browsers.

It is easily modifiable and rebrandable and has a minimal overhead ensuring a slick experience on any platform.
